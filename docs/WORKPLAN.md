Produce the strongest evolved titanium utility karambit design that satisfies **all 16 `W_CAD_v2` engineering gates** (`ops/ci/evaluate_cad_candidate.py`) **and** **all 10 `RS-karambit-v2` hard-gate groups** (`requirements/RS-karambit-v2.toml`, `crates/cli/src/bench.rs`), generated end-to-end by a fully automated, reproducible `./evolve.sh` (`jarambit auto-evolve`) pipeline with independent dual-solver 3D FEM (`scikit-fem` $P_2$ + containerized `DOLFINx` v0.9.0 $P_2$ on the identical saved fine TET10 mesh) and OpenCascade (`BRepCheck_Analyzer`) B-rep verification. ### Hard Scientific & Operational Invariants
1. **No threshold or gate relaxation:** Every threshold in `requirements/RS-karambit-v1.toml`, `requirements/RS-karambit-v2.toml`, `crates/crescent/src/{gates,fem,aesthetic,certification,export}.rs`, and `ops/ci/evaluate_cad_candidate.py` is frozen. Missing or unverified observations fail closed (`unverified` / `false`), never count as met, and never populate `champion`.
2. **Honest certification state (`T02`):** Until an evolved genome exported on fixed `main` achieves `all_16_gates == true` and `rs_v2_all_hard == true` with committed raw solver sidecars and hashes, `qualified_count = 0` and `champion = null`. No hand-built reference CAD (`c10a`, `c93`, `c92`, `c10c`) or pre-#82 historical screen record (`w282_p14_c01`) may be substituted for an evolved post-#84 certified winner.
3. **One integration PR at a time:** Parallel lanes work in isolated worktrees with bounded file ownership and explicit receipts, feeding one integration PR into `main`. Merge requires `neverhumanbot` approval on the **exact** head SHA and `gh-role writer pr merge <n> --repo neverhuman/jarambit --rebase --match-head-commit <sha>` with green `version-check` and `d1-gates`.
