\subsection{Plate Fields and Assessment Stages}
\label{subsec:family-lateral}
\label{subsec:family-ring}
\label{subsec:family-torsion}
For Individual~2000, the retained plate mesh contains $13{,}503$ nodes and $25{,}209$ triangles. Lateral bending and chord torsion use DKT bending; in-plane tip pry and ring traction use quadratic T6 membrane elements. Their unit-response maps have distinct units and load definitions (Section~\ref{sec:verification}). The scaled multi-case envelope is derived from those maps; it is not an independent fifth load case.

The assessment comparison in Supplement Figure~\ref{S-fig:assessment}C is \emph{certification versus strict}, not a generic coarse/refined pair. Certification takes the minimum capacity over maximum triangle areas $0.25$ and $0.125\,\mathrm{mm}^2$, while strict assessment uses $0.0625\,\mathrm{mm}^2$. The saved lateral capacities are $168.90$ and $168.18\,\mathrm{N}$; the local tip-torsion factors are $0.524$ and $0.529$. The displayed fields come from the $0.25\,\mathrm{mm}^2$ level. These stages are distinct from the $4/1/0.25\,\mathrm{mm}^2$ plate mesh study.

The global envelope excludes cells within $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ of the tip. The plate body-torsion observable searches beyond $15\,\mathrm{mm}$ from the tip; the local tip observable searches the $4$--$15\,\mathrm{mm}$ annulus. Raw torsion imagery retains the tip-patch response and must distinguish its raw peak from the post-exclusion capacity peak. The ring-case producer masks a half-plane beginning $3\,\mathrm{mm}$ before the seat; it includes the seat and unloaded blade. Those cells are masked rather than interpreted as measured zero stress.

\subsection{Local Solid Response and Recovery}
\label{subsec:family-solid}
\label{subsec:family-verification}
The retained $P_2$ solid submodel uses $91{,}008$ tetrahedra and $416{,}583$ displacement degrees of freedom. Its cut-arc displacement degrees of freedom are clamped, and vertex forces in a $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ tip patch apply $1.82\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ chord torque. The retained comparison is scikit-fem/PyAMG CG versus DOLFINx/PETSc CG/GAMG on that same saved submodel. Rust orchestration is not the primary solid assembly implementation. Section~\ref{sec:verification} specifies the common inputs, recovery and selected agreement; the publication inputs contain scalar and binned profiles, rather than a full three-dimensional stress field.

\subsection{Proxies, Support Assumptions and Missing Observations}
\label{subsec:family-support}
\label{subsec:family-sequence}
\label{subsec:family-fatigue}
The regional yield-order ratio compares modeled elastic capacities and has a stored target of $1.2$. It does not model plastic redistribution, cracking or a physical failure sequence. Likewise, the modified-Goodman fatigue factor ($n_f=0.472$) is a scalar model result, not an observed cycle life.

An alternate support study uses bore-bearing springs and a Winkler grip-pad foundation. Its lateral regional ratio ($5.203$) remains separate from the certification multi-case ratio ($1.435$); these differing support/load observables are not a mesh-convergence pair. The original buckling value and pass flag are preserved as historical records, but are withdrawn from interpretation as an admitted buckling-performance result. The producer applies a symmetric eigensolver to $\mathbf K_s^{-1}\mathbf G$ without a retained operator-admissibility check or eigenpair residual. No replacement eigenvalue or rescored fitness is introduced. Supplement Figure~\ref{S-fig:assessment}A supplies historical support bookkeeping, not a validated stability margin; no support eigenvector field is retained.

\subsection{Geometry Records and Control Catalogs}
\label{subsec:family-geometry}
\label{subsec:family-reference}
\label{subsec:family-controls}
Export identities and reference-contour comparisons bind what was generated and measured. They establish neither physical performance nor human comfort. The historical $23\times18$ battery and 44-entry crosswalk remain available in the Supplement. They mix candidate-specific observations, shared software controls, missing bindings and completeness flags. In particular, its 20 quadratic-solid completeness labels do not denote 20 favorable strength margins. Catalog definitions, executed controls and unavailable records are reported separately; their counts are not a mechanical replication denominator. The stored \code{plate\_gci\_valid\_3level} predicate is historical status bookkeeping: its favorable flag does not establish an admitted extrapolated error estimate when the retained GCI procedure refuses the sequences. Original predicate names and outcomes remain unchanged.
