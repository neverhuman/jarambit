\section{Numerical Observation Families and Methodological Evolution}
\label{sec:battery}
\IEEEPARstart{E}{valuating} an evolved physical geometry requires translating continuous shape variations into concrete geometric and mechanical observables. Earlier internal documentation listed forty-four catalog predicates---sixteen legacy diagnostic checks, ten grouped requirement predicates, and eighteen historical battery columns---inline alongside a 414-cell categorical status matrix. While complete, that administrative taxonomy obscured the physical and numerical methods.

In this section, we organize the main-text presentation around the \textbf{eleven substantive observation and model families} defined by the underlying physics and software architecture (Table~\ref{tab:observation-families}). Every subsection follows a uniform seven-part structure: (1)~engineering question, (2)~represented observable and units, (3)~numerical domain and formulation, (4)~role across historical and weighted objective regimes, (5)~retained visual evidence for the assessed export Individual~2000, (6)~interpretation limits, and (7)~cross-reference to the Supplement technical crosswalk (Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}). The complete 23-subject by 18-evaluator historical battery matrix (\BatteryPass\ stored passes, \BatteryFail\ failures, and \BatteryBlocked\ blocked cells; preserved in the linked Supplement \nolinkurl{generated/battery-matrix.svg} and Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}) and the item-by-item 44-entry crosswalk are preserved without omission.

\subsection{Gate and Objective Evolution Across Regimes}
\label{subsec:gate-evolution}
Understanding the retained data requires distinguishing three evaluation regimes that coexist in the repository without conflating their semantics:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Historical Native Search (Cohort~1 \text{Run~1}, Cohort~3 \text{Run~2}--\text{Run~11}, and Four-Island Quality-Diversity):} During native quality-diversity evolution, each birth is decoded and checked sequentially for geometric legality, reference-contour similarity, and plate finite-element feasibility. The historical scalar fitness $f$ is therefore \emph{piecewise and conditional}: candidates failing geometric legality or the reference-contour floor receive negative penalty branches without invoking FEM (\FemNotEvaluated\ of \BirthCount\ births in Cohort~1 \text{Run~1}), whereas evaluated candidates combine normalized structural response ratios, mass terms, and archive-contextual novelty. Removing the categorical pass/fail matrix from the opening narrative does not make the historical search algorithm gate-free; its conditional branch structure is preserved as an explicit methodological fact.
  \item \textbf{Multi-Stage Screening and Retained Weighted Reporting Profile:} In the intermediate pipeline, Stage~1 screens candidates using fast plate and geometric observables, while subsequent evaluation adds local quadratic-solid and refined observations. The 20-term retained weighted reporting profile $F = \sum_{i=1}^{20} w_i s_i \in [0,100]$ aggregates four thematic groups (30\% mass/compactness, 25\% form/comfort proxies, 15\% exported geometry, 30\% response/verification). Prior to repository revision \code{72e25a360470}, selection and reporting differed on two placeholder geometry inputs, and round maxima combined stage values across rounds. We therefore keep historical native $f$, Stage-1 screen scores, and the retained weighted reporting profile strictly separate.
  \item \textbf{Fixed Historical Assessment Battery (23 Subjects $\times$ 18 Columns):} The historical CAD battery evaluates 23 fixed subjects (21 earlier-cohort subjects and two controlled-probe exports including Individual~2001) across 18 heterogeneous columns mixing shared software controls (92 of the \BatteryPass\ passes), missing geometry-binding receipts (\BatteryBlocked\ blocked cells across unit-declaration, single-solid, and pin-clearance checks plus four reference rows), record-completeness flags (20 quadratic-solid file-completeness records), and strict grouped requirement gates (19 failures and 4 blocked reference rows).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Family 1: Representation and Exported Geometry}
\label{subsec:family-geometry}
\textbf{Question \& Observables:} Does a decoded genome produce a closed, single-solid B-rep and surface mesh with verifiable unit scaling, mass ($\mathrm{g}$), edge/tip profile geometry ($\mathrm{mm}$), and cryptographic byte binding?
\textbf{Domain \& Formulation:} The decoder maps the 128-gene vector and material density into a 2D mid-surface contour, variable thickness/bevel fields, and 3D STEP/STL exports.
\textbf{Objective Role:} Geometric legality gates initial evaluation in native QD search; exported geometry terms contribute 15\% of the retained weighted reporting profile.
\textbf{Retained Visual \& Limits:} Figure~\ref{fig:export-identity} binds Individual~2000's genome, STEP/STL digests, 1 solid / 1 shell / 55,698 STEP face tokens, OpenCascade valid B-rep record, and 44,994-vertex / 90,000-triangle Blender render mesh. Three geometry checks in this family (holder-envelope clearance, ring/claw planform proportions, and B-rep edge/tip metrology) intentionally omit synthetic subsection visuals (4 entries total across the 44-entry catalog) because nominal parameters and exported B-rep metrology must not be conflated. In the historical battery, unit-declaration, single-solid, and pin-clearance bindings are blocked across all 23 subjects (Supplement Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}).

\subsection{Family 2: Shape and Reference Comparison}
\label{subsec:family-reference}
\textbf{Question \& Observables:} How closely does the decoded exterior silhouette match the authored reference archetype in Hausdorff distance ($\mathrm{mm}$), curvature-histogram Earth Mover's Distance (EMD, dimensionless), and composite similarity score $\in [0,1]$?
\textbf{Domain \& Formulation:} Evaluates the closed 2D exterior polyline (4,230 points for Individual~2000) against the retained C.5 exterior reference polyline (1,117 points, reused by C.6) under a common isotropic coordinate transform.
\textbf{Objective Role:} Enforces the reference-contour similarity floor before FEM in native QD search and supplies form/reference proxy terms in weighted reporting.
\textbf{Retained Visual \& Limits:} Figure~\ref{fig:reference} displays both polylines and the saved descriptors (Hausdorff $2.285\,\mathrm{mm}$, curvature EMD $0.0071$, similarity score $0.4079$, empty failure list). Reference resemblance measures conformity to an authored geometric prior, not human ergonomic approval or comfort (Supplement Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}).

\subsection{Family 3: Plate Lateral and Pry Response}
\label{subsec:family-lateral}
\textbf{Question \& Observables:} What are the linear-elastic unit responses under out-of-plane lateral tip load ($\mathrm{MPa/N}$) and in-plane tip-pry load ($\mathrm{MPa/N}$), and the resulting lateral load capacity $F_{\mathrm{lat}}$ ($\mathrm{N}$) across coarse, certification, and strict plate discretizations?
\textbf{Domain \& Formulation:} Discrete Kirchhoff Triangle (DKT) out-of-plane bending elements and six-node quadratic membrane (T6) in-plane bending elements on the 2D mid-surface ($13{,}503$ nodes and $25{,}209$ triangles at the $0.25\,\mathrm{mm}^2$ level; minima taken over $0.25$ and $0.125\,\mathrm{mm}^2$ for certification, and $0.0625\,\mathrm{mm}^2$ for strict refinement). In the multi-case envelope construction, both lateral bending and tip-pry unit-response fields are multiplied by $260\,\mathrm{N}$.
\textbf{Objective Role:} Primary structural capacity term in native QD fitness and the response/verification group (30\%) of the retained weighted reporting profile.
\textbf{Retained Visual \& Limits:} Figure~\ref{fig:plate-fields}B shows the $0.25\,\mathrm{mm}^2$ lateral unit-response field ($25{,}209$ valid triangles), while Fig.~\ref{fig:plate-fields}A shows the scaled multi-case envelope ($25{,}134$ valid triangles, with $75$ tip-exclusion cells masked in gray; peak $1{,}388.6695\,\mathrm{MPa}$). Figure~\ref{fig:assessment}C records the certification ($168.9008\,\mathrm{N}$) and strict ($168.1786\,\mathrm{N}$) lateral capacities against the $180\,\mathrm{N}$ coarse and $260\,\mathrm{N}$ certification/strict thresholds, and no full spatial field is archived for the $0.0625\,\mathrm{mm}^2$ strict mesh (Supplement Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}).

\subsection{Family 4: Ring Traction}
\label{subsec:family-ring}
\textbf{Question \& Observables:} What is the in-plane membrane von Mises stress per unit ring-pull resultant ($\mathrm{MPa/N}$) and the static ring-pull capacity ($\mathrm{N}$)?
\textbf{Domain \& Formulation:} Six-node quadratic membrane triangles (T6) on the mid-surface under static ring-bore traction. In the retained $25{,}209$-triangle plate mesh, $14{,}875$ cells in the seated handle/blade region are producer-zeroed, leaving $10{,}334$ active triangles in the ring and immediate transition domain.
\textbf{Objective Role:} Contributes to the joint structural floor in native search and multi-stage response scoring.
\textbf{Retained Visual \& Limits:} Figure~\ref{fig:plate-fields}C renders the $10{,}334$ valid triangles and explicitly masks the $14{,}875$ producer-zeroed seated cells in neutral gray so excluded domain is never misread as zero stress. Static elastic ring traction is not a dynamic drop test or human-finger injury model (Supplement Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}).

\subsection{Family 5: Body and Local Plate Torsion}
\label{subsec:family-torsion}
\textbf{Question \& Observables:} How do the body region and terminal tip region respond to a $1.82\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ torsional load in unit response ($\mathrm{MPa/(N\,mm)}$) and regional safety factors ($\mathrm{SF}$, dimensionless)?
\textbf{Domain \& Formulation:} DKT plate torsion model with distinct regional masks: body torsion excludes a $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ terminal tip zone ($75$ cells at $0.25\,\mathrm{mm}^2$) when computing body capacity and the global envelope, whereas local tip torsion evaluates the terminal zone at certification ($0.25/0.125\,\mathrm{mm}^2$) and strict ($0.0625\,\mathrm{mm}^2$) levels.
\textbf{Objective Role:} Separates global blade/handle torsional stiffness from localized tip fragility across both search and assessment stages.
\textbf{Retained Visual \& Limits:} Figure~\ref{fig:plate-fields}D shows the unmasked raw torsion unit response across all $25{,}209$ triangles with the $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ capacity-exclusion boundary dashed; Fig.~\ref{fig:assessment}C pairs the certification ($0.5239$) and strict ($0.5287$) local tip safety factors. Two-dimensional Kirchhoff plate theory cannot capture three-dimensional through-thickness shear near a sharp point, motivating Family~6 (Supplement Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}).

\subsection{Family 6: Quadratic Solid Submodel}
\label{subsec:family-solid}
\textbf{Question \& Observables:} What is the three-dimensional continuum safety factor ($\mathrm{SF}_{\mathrm{solid}}$, dimensionless) in the terminal tip region under $1.82\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ torsion?
\textbf{Domain \& Formulation:} Ten-node quadratic tetrahedral ($P_2$) continuum submodel of the terminal tip region, recording nodal-maximum and regionally binned safety factors.
\textbf{Objective Role:} High-fidelity tip verification anchor.
\textbf{Retained Visual \& Limits:} Figure~\ref{fig:verification}C--D plots the matched-mesh scalar safety factors and regional longitudinal profiles. No full 3D volumetric stress field array is retained in the publication inputs. Furthermore, in the historical 23-subject battery, the quadratic-solid tip column records 20 passes that signify \emph{record completeness}, not 20 favorable strength margins (Supplement Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}).

\subsection{Family 7: Compliant Support and Linear Stability}
\label{subsec:family-support}
\textbf{Question \& Observables:} Under compliant bore-bearing springs and a Winkler grip-pad foundation, what are the lateral regional stress ratio and the lowest linear buckling eigenvalue $\lambda_1$ (dimensionless)?
\textbf{Domain \& Formulation:} Alternate DKT/CST assembly solving static compliant support (reported ratio $5.2027$ vs.\ multi-case certification comparator $1.4353$) and linear eigenbuckling ($\lambda_1 = 7.0351$) via Krylov iteration on $\mathbf{K}_s^{-1}\mathbf{G}$.
\textbf{Objective Role:} Side-report stability diagnostic in assessment and the historical battery.
\textbf{Retained Visual \& Limits:} Figure~\ref{fig:assessment}A presents the support schematic and archived scalars. Because standard Hermitian Lanczos iteration requires an operator self-adjoint in the inner product and symmetric $\mathbf{K}_s, \mathbf{G}$ do not make $\mathbf{K}_s^{-1}\mathbf{G}$ Euclidean-symmetric without a $\mathbf{K}_s$-inner-product formulation or retained eigenpair residual, the numerical-operator audit remains explicitly open (Supplement Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}).

\subsection{Family 8: Regional Yield-Order Proxy}
\label{subsec:family-sequence}
\textbf{Question \& Observables:} Does the modeled elastic response ratio between designated body/ring and tip regions ($r_{\mathrm{seq}}$, dimensionless) exceed the $1.2$ ordering threshold?
\textbf{Domain \& Formulation:} Quotient of regional linear-elastic capacity factors evaluated across the plate mesh sequence.
\textbf{Objective Role:} Penalizes tip-first elastic overload in native search and weighted reporting.
\textbf{Retained Visual \& Limits:} Figure~\ref{fig:verification}A plots the sequence proxy across the three plate mesh levels. Because linear elasticity models neither plastic redistribution, crack initiation, nor dynamic fracture propagation, a favorable sequence ratio is an elastic design proxy rather than a proven physical failure sequence (Supplement Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}).

\subsection{Family 9: Fatigue Model}
\label{subsec:family-fatigue}
\textbf{Question \& Observables:} What is the modified-Goodman endurance safety factor $n_f$ (dimensionless) under a repeated zero-to-maximum load cycle?
\textbf{Domain \& Formulation:} Evaluates $n_f^{-1} = \sigma_a / S_e + \sigma_m / S_u$ with $\sigma_a = \sigma_m = \sigma_{\max}/2$ and Marin-corrected Ti-6Al-4V endurance limit $S_e$.
\textbf{Objective Role:} Durability screen (saved scalar $n_f = 0.4718$ for Individual~2000; 19 unfavorable and 4 blocked in the historical battery).
\textbf{Retained Visual \& Limits:} Figure~\ref{fig:assessment}B displays the saved scalar $0.4718$. No spatial fatigue field is stored, and nominal Goodman factors do not substitute for surface-roughness metrology, residual-stress measurement, or cyclic coupon testing (Supplement Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}).

\subsection{Family 10: Discretization and Separate-Solver Consistency}
\label{subsec:family-verification}
\textbf{Question \& Observables:} Do three-level plate mesh sequences admit an asymptotic Grid Convergence Index (GCI), and do independent finite-element implementations agree on the matched $P_2$ solid mesh?
\textbf{Domain \& Formulation:} Three-level Richardson extrapolation and refusal predicates plus matched-mesh $P_2$ tetrahedral solves comparing the native solver against FEniCSx/DOLFINx.
\textbf{Objective Role:} Verification and credibility gates (Section~\ref{sec:verification}).
\textbf{Retained Visual \& Limits:} Figure~\ref{fig:verification}A--B shows that while mesh-sequence metadata are valid, oscillatory and nonpositive observed orders cause the GCI error estimates to be refused. Figure~\ref{fig:verification}C shows that the two solvers on the identical $P_2$ mesh agree within $0.05\%$, confirming solver-implementation parity on a shared discretization without bounding discretization or modeling error (Supplement Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}).

\subsection{Family 11: Continuous Objective Subscores and Result-Completeness Controls}
\label{subsec:family-controls}
\textbf{Question \& Observables:} How do continuous CAD quality subscores and shortfall magnitudes combine into $W_{\text{CAD}}$, and do unit/invariance tests, GCI refusal arithmetic, claim-admission gates, and historical side-report references behave as specified?
\textbf{Domain \& Formulation:} Continuous subscore and shortfall evaluation plus automated positive and negative software fixtures across the historical battery columns.
\textbf{Objective Role:} Separates continuous design quality from gate shortfalls and fail-closed repository governance; software-control passes are never weighted as mechanical fitness.
\textbf{Retained Visual \& Limits:} Figure~\ref{fig:assessment}D presents the five normalized CAD quality subscores ($s_{\mathrm{mass}}=0.4870$, $s_{\mathrm{karambit}}=0.4009$, $s_{\mathrm{survival}}=0.0$, $s_{\mathrm{seq}}=0.9996$, $s_{\mathrm{carry}}=0.6000$; pass-branch unpenalized quality score $1.4570$) alongside the three continuous gate shortfalls ($0.4710$ refined tip torsion, $0.3531$ refined plate bending, and $0.3307$ quadratic solid tip; conditional failed-branch formula $W_{\text{CAD}} = -1.0 - 4.6191 + 0.10 s_{\mathrm{mass}} = -5.5704$; 10 of 16 legacy gates and 2 of 10 requirement groups met) for Individual~2000, while the linked Supplement (\nolinkurl{generated/battery-matrix.svg} and \nolinkurl{generated/battery-matrix.json}, Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}) retains all 414 historical cells across the 23 battery subjects (\BatteryPass\ stored passes, \BatteryFail\ failures, and \BatteryBlocked\ blocked records), explaining why 92 repeated software-control passes across four build columns and 57 shared side-study references must be separated from candidate-specific mechanical tests.
