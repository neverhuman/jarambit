\section{Introduction: Engineered Intelligence as an Executable Design Process}
\label{sec:introduction}
\IEEEPARstart{E}{ngineering} design is traditionally documented through its terminal artifact: a finished CAD assembly, a selected stress contour, and a summary table asserting that requirements were met. When evolutionary computation and automated numerical modeling enter the loop, that artifact-only view conceals the primary scientific object. The primary object is no longer a single hand-tuned shape, but an executable design process that generates, evaluates, retains, and relates candidate geometries under explicit numerical preferences.

We define \emph{Engineered Intelligence} operationally through five coupled components: a versioned numerical representation, a deterministic geometric decoder, a recorded variation and selection loop, declared stage-specific objectives, and an inspectable archive of every evaluated candidate and observation. Defining the discipline operationally avoids both overclaiming autonomous invention and allowing test-catalog administration to obscure how candidate geometries actually evolve.

\subsection{The Executable-Representation Mechanism}
\label{subsec:executable-mechanism}
Jarambit instantiates this architecture on a monolithic titanium karambit structural design problem. Every candidate is encoded by 128 continuous floating-point coordinates paired with one categorical alloy state (\code{Ti-6Al-4V} or \code{Ti-3Al-2.5V}), forming a 129-field genome record. Decoding a genome maps 40 normal spline offsets around a closed reference outline, global planform and ring dimensions, stock-to-tip taper and bevel angles, and conditional interior pocketing or continuous-rail thickness laws into a watertight B-rep solid, a surface mesh, and a 2.5D plate/membrane or 3D quadratic tetrahedral finite-element discretization. Within each evolutionary run, four subpopulations (Islands~1--4) apply block-preserving silhouette crossover and six structured mutation modes to generate offspring, evaluate their geometric legality and mechanical response, update a behavioral quality-diversity archive, and periodically migrate elites around an island ring.

\subsection{Research Question and Three-Cohort Empirical Findings}
\label{subsec:research-question-and-results}
The central research question of this study is: \emph{How do representation constraints, contextual search objectives, and multi-fidelity numerical verifiers interact across complete evolutionary populations, and why must search-stage preference remain separated from engineering qualification?}

Because historical search runs, multi-stage screening waves, and long-horizon quality-diversity campaigns used distinct scorer revisions and retention policies, we analyze the empirical record across three separate cohorts without pooling incompatible scores into a synthetic grand total:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Cohort~3 (10-Run 800-Gen Population, 1,954,560 Evaluations --- \text{Run~2} through \text{Run~11}):} Ten retained four-island runs completed 801 generations ($0$--$800$) with 195,456 evaluated births per run, providing 1,954,560 complete 146-column records (17 scalar evaluation metrics plus all 129 genome-and-material fields) and 32,040 generation-by-island summary rows. Across these ten retained runs, 754,236 evaluations ($38.59\%$; per-run mean $75{,}423.6$, sample SD $2{,}936.55$, population SD $2{,}785.86$) satisfy search-level geometric legality, and run-maximum native quality-diversity fitness reaches between $1.1089$ (\text{Run~3}) and $1.1440$ (\text{Run~5}) (mean $1.1250$, sample SD $0.01285$, population SD $0.01220$). An eleventh staged summary cohort (\text{Run~12}--\text{Run~22}, 17,672 generation-by-island summary rows reaching generation~708) records earlier summary-only trajectories.
  \item \textbf{Cohort~1 (Historical 17-Gen Genealogy --- \text{Run~1}):} A fully reconstructable 17-generation four-island experiment retains 1,984 births (1,455 with completed plate finite-element solves, 529 not evaluated by finite elements, and 446 search-legal births), 2,176 island-slot membership occurrences ($4\times 32\times 17$, including 192 elite reappearances), 128 seed births, 938 mutation births, 918 crossover births, and 12 ring-migration events at generations 4, 8, and 12. Tracing the three-hop ancestry of the highest-scoring birth (Individual~1095 at generation~9, native fitness $1.15727$) reveals ancestor reuse across branches (Individual~413 and Individual~174) and shows that Individual~1095 fails subsequent strict regional yield-order sequence verification.
  \item \textbf{Cohort~2 (Intermediate Multi-Stage Screening):} Across 24 multi-stage screening runs, 3,649 birth proposals are retained, of which 2,259 link via validated one-based candidate identity to Stage-1 screening scores and 1,390 remain in an explicit unscreened observation band rather than being zero-filled or conflated with solver failures. Within the six retained ultralight reporting curves (15 generation summaries), Individual~2002 records the highest retained weighted reporting profile score ($74.207299$ on the $0$--$100$ scale, display loss $25.792701$), while separately assessed controlled exports Individual~2001 ($W_{\text{CAD}} = -3.878704$, $G_1 = 0.623903$, mass $75.382\,\mathrm{g}$, $11$ of $16$ legacy gates met and $5$ failed) and Individual~2007 ($W_{\text{CAD}} = -3.964924$, $G_1 = 0.621634$, mass $75.642\,\mathrm{g}$, $11$ of $16$ legacy gates met and $5$ failed) fail strict multi-gate verification.
\end{enumerate}

For detailed spatial and multi-solver mechanics, we examine the illustrated assessed geometry Individual~2000 (the single assessed export in the automatic certification leaderboard, 1 assessed candidate, 0 qualified, no promoted champion; separate from the 23-subject historical battery, while the $1{,}954{,}560$ native search births lack retained Stage-2 CAD assessments). Its retained $0.25\,\mathrm{mm}^2$ plate discretization ($25{,}209$ triangles, $13{,}503$ nodes across seven retained mesh arrays) and $91{,}008$-element quadratic tetrahedral tip submodel ($416{,}583$ displacement degrees of freedom) demonstrate $0.04\%$--$0.05\%$ cross-solver agreement between independent finite-element engines while exposing non-monotonic and negative-order plate grid-convergence behavior that must be refused rather than smoothed over.

\subsection{Bounded Contributions and Provenance Scope}
\label{subsec:contributions}
This paper makes four bounded contributions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Executable Genome and Variation Exposition (Sections~\ref{sec:genome}--\ref{sec:evolution}):} We document the exact 129-field genome contract, decoder clamping couplings, block-preserving silhouette crossover, and six-mode mutation operators, linking them to recorded multi-hop genealogies and coordinate-level parent-child differences.
  \item \textbf{Full-Population Archival Census Across Three Cohorts (Sections~\ref{sec:evolution} and~\ref{sec:results}):} We present the complete 1,954,560-evaluation ten-run generation-800 population alongside the 1,984-birth historical genealogy and 3,649 intermediate screening proposals, maintaining strict separation between birth clouds, island memberships, and stage-specific score profiles.
  \item \textbf{Source-Bound Multi-Model Mechanics and Saliency Visualization (Sections~\ref{sec:fitness}--\ref{sec:verification}):} We define area-weighted midrank percentile composites across the five structural demand maps (multi-case von Mises envelope and unit lateral, pry, ring-traction, and torsion responses) and $\ge 90\%$ integrated-saliency local crops over the retained plate fields of Individual~2000, while moving the exhaustive 414-cell historical battery matrix and 44-entry technical identifier crosswalk to the Supplement.
  \item \textbf{Compact Lossless Population Archive Specification (Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}):} We specify and verify a segmented zstd population archive format ($200$ segments compressing $3.81\,\mathrm{GB}$ of raw CSV records into $494.8\,\mathrm{MB}$ with $100\%$ round-trip identity) that separates historical reconstruction revisions, observed repository checkouts, and executed binary provenance.
\end{enumerate}

\emph{Scope and Non-Claims:} All results reported here are retrospective computational records; each cohort is attributed to its specific producer revision in Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility} rather than attributed to a single uniform binary. No candidate in any cohort is physically qualified, no hardware was machined or mechanically tested, and no claim is made of fleet-wide completion (10 of 18 native run directories reached generation~800) or autonomous representation design.
