\section{Evolutionary Process and Population Evidence}
\subsection{Existing Lanes and Stages}
Jarambit contains several evolutionary paths. The automatic lane constructs offspring, performs native screening, ranks candidates, selects a subset for more expensive independent assessment, and retains reports. The crescent quality-diversity lane and the older NSGA-II/Monte Carlo lane have their own representations and evaluation conventions. They should be described by their actual entry points and source revisions rather than folded into a single generic algorithm.

The current automatic implementation is in \src{crates/cli/src/auto_evolve.rs}{auto\_evolve.rs}. Its native Stage 1 and Stage 2 scoring functions differ from the separate reporting scorer. Stage 1 supplies a cheaper screen. Stage 2 invokes the independent assessment path for selected candidates. Selection and qualification remain conceptually distinct: a candidate may be worth retaining for research while lacking the observations or findings required for qualification.

The v2 release describes weighted, non-veto ranking in the selection path. This statement should not be extended into a claim that every possible genome reaches every evaluator, that invalid computations are acceptable evidence, or that all historical search lanes are veto-free. Source paths still determine constructibility, runtime availability, and which observations exist. Evidence validity cannot be inferred from a policy about selection preference.

\subsection{Four Different Collections}
A birth collection records proposed or evaluated candidates. A survivor collection records candidates retained after selection. An archive records selected representatives under an archive policy. An independent-assessment collection records candidates admitted to an expensive evaluation stage. These sets can overlap while having different denominators and sampling biases. A figure needs to name which set it uses.

The selected w411c3 retained leaderboard contains six assessed entries. That is not the full birth population. Its curve contains three generation summaries, but the referenced round-screen files are unavailable. The reporting producer substitutes an assessment score when a matching label exists and otherwise uses a screening/proxy score. Point-level stage lineage cannot be reconstructed here. We therefore display the retained summaries without treating them as a homogeneous objective-convergence experiment or inferring a birth distribution.

By contrast, the historical w282 \code{all_evals.json} contains \BirthCount\ retained births with explicit identifiers and generations. Of these, \FemComplete\ report completed FEM, \FemNotEvaluated\ have \code{fem_status=not_evaluated}, and \LegalBirths\ satisfy the recorded search-level legality classification. The figure retains every stored fitness value, including penalties for cases that never reached FEM. It is not a plot of \code{W_ULTRALIGHT_v1}, nor does every point represent a mechanics solve.

The original provenance pins producer revision \code{82f450f7fb41}. Its \href{https://github.com/neverhuman/jarambit/blob/82f450f7fb418e28c4b229031920dec5454435f3/crates/crescent/src/qd.rs}{quality-diversity implementation} recomputes novelty using archive/current-population context and includes it in the score. Fitness is also branched by legality. A saved value is therefore a contextual search observation, not a fixed physical property of its genome. The publication reader admits the recorded producer identity and verifies population/provenance/summary hashes against the original wave manifest.

\subsection{Best-So-Far Is an Archive Property}
For a fixed higher-is-better metric, let $r_g$ be the best observed value in generation $g$. A retained Hall of Fame reports
\begin{equation}
 H_g=\max(H_{g-1},r_g).
\end{equation}
This recurrence guarantees a non-decreasing stored envelope if the archive is preserved and the metric meaning is unchanged. It does not guarantee that the next generation improves, that the median improves, that a physical constraint is met, or that the search approaches a global optimum. A plateau can coexist with continued exploration or with a stalled representation.

For a lower-is-better objective, the corresponding envelope uses a minimum. Merely reversing the display direction of a score does not change the underlying tradeoffs. More importantly, changing an objective, decoder, model, or measurement convention breaks the simple interpretation of one continuous envelope. A version boundary belongs on the plot or requires a separate panel.

\subsection{Population Statistics and Missingness}
The generation median is computed from sorted finite retained observations; for an even sample count, it is the average of the two central values. The generation maximum is computed within the same generation membership. No interpolation supplies absent births, and no assumed zero replaces a missing score. Unique birth identifiers are checked before the reduction.

Fitness spread is not the same as geometric diversity. A population can contain many shapes with similar scores or many distinct scores from nearly identical shapes. The historical repository also retains genotype and descriptor analyses, but those measures depend on normalization and the active gene set. The current paper's plot makes the narrower statement that the recorded scalar distribution changes over generations. It does not use that distribution as proof of representation coverage.

\subsection{What Scale Would Need to Show}
Increasing compute can increase the number of candidate evaluations, but the benefit depends on evaluator cost, representation, caching, scheduling, and the fraction of useful observations. This paper has no controlled compute-scaling experiment. It therefore reports no speedup, evaluations-per-hour result, or evidence that massive compute will reliably drive the score toward an ideal.

A credible future scaling study would distinguish proposal count, successful numerical evaluations, independent assessments, and useful retained alternatives, while holding the scientific question and evidence rules fixed. Until such measurements exist, scale remains a system ambition. The immediate methodological requirement is that larger runs preserve the provenance and failure information needed to understand what their increased activity actually produced.
