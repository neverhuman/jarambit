\section{Evolutionary Process, Variation Operators, and Population Evidence}
\label{sec:evolution}

\subsection{Implemented Variation Operators: Block-Preserving Crossover and Six-Mode Mutation}
Rather than treating evolutionary search as an opaque black-box optimizer, Jarambit records the exact variation operators applied across its island populations:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Two-parent crossover:} To prevent non-physical saw-tooth discontinuities along the closed silhouette spline, the 40 normal control points (\code{cp00}--\code{cp39}, indices $0..39$) are inherited as a single contiguous block from parent~$b$ with probability $0.5$ and otherwise retained from parent~$a$. The remaining 88 numeric feature coordinates (indices $40..127$) and the categorical alloy grade (\code{material}) are inherited independently with probability $0.5$ per coordinate, followed immediately by deterministic clamping.
\item \textbf{Six-mode structured mutation:} Each mutation call draws an integer mode $\text{op}\in\{0,1,2,3,4,5\}$ uniformly at random with step scale $\sigma=0.15$ (coarse) or $\sigma=0.04$ (fine):
  \begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{Mode~0 (Sparse per-gene Gaussian):} Perturbs each coordinate $i\in[0,127]$ independently with probability $0.15$ by $\mathcal{N}(0,1)\cdot(\text{hi}_i-\text{lo}_i)\sigma$.
  \item \emph{Mode~1 (Smooth silhouette bump):} Applies a periodic Gaussian normal displacement bump $A\exp(-d^2/(2w^2))$ across all 40 control points around a random station $c\in[0,39]$ with width $w\in[1.5,5.5]$.
  \item \emph{Mode~2 (Thickness, taper, and relief operator):} Perturbs stock thickness $t_{\text{stock}}$, tip thickness $t_{\text{tip}}$, taper origin $x_{0,\text{taper}}$, and handle relief depth and width, and---when $p_{1,\text{on}} \ge 0.5$---also perturbs perimeter rail width $w_{\text{rail}}$, pocket depth $d_{\text{pocket}}$, and one interior spline knot \code{k0}--\code{k4}.
  \item \emph{Mode~3 (Dimple-lattice operator):} Deactivates the lattice ($d_{\text{lattice}} = 0$) with probability $0.15$ or perturbs lattice depth, pitch, fill fraction, angle, and handle/blade extents ($68..73$).
  \item \emph{Mode~4 (Fuller, flute, texture, and comet operator):} Perturbs blade fuller and handle flute geometry ($74..78$), surface texture mode and pitch/angle ($79..82$), and comet groove width/bow ($52..54$).
  \item \emph{Mode~5 (Internal pocketing or continuous-rail operator):} When $p_{1,\text{on}} \ge 0.5$, mutates the continuous perimeter rail and knot coordinates ($118..127$) with probability $0.7$; otherwise selects one of four sub-operators targeting triangular isogrid ($90..98$), pierced windows ($99..104$), ring flute ($105..109$), or angled micro-perforations ($110..116$).
  \end{enumerate}
  In coarse mode, the alloy grade flips between \code{Ti-6Al-4V} and \code{Ti-3Al-2.5V} with probability $0.03$ before clamping.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Four Distinct Population Collections}
Scientifically credible reporting requires distinguishing four collections that share candidates but have different denominators and selection biases:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Birth collection:} Every proposed candidate evaluated or rejected at birth:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Cohort~3 (10-Run 800-Gen Population, $1{,}954{,}560$ Evaluations):} \text{Run~2} through \text{Run~11} each evaluate $195{,}456$ births across generations $0$--$800$ ($801$ distinct generations per run), totaling $1{,}954{,}560$ complete 146-column birth records ($754{,}236$ search-legal).
  \item \emph{Cohort~1 (Historical 17-Gen, \text{Run~1}):} $1{,}984$ births across generations $0$--$16$ ($1{,}455$ with completed plate finite-element solves, $529$ not evaluated by finite elements, and $446$ search-legal).
  \item \emph{Cohort~2 (Intermediate Screening):} $3{,}649$ multi-stage birth proposals across 24 screening runs, with $2{,}259$ joined to Stage-1 screening observations and $1{,}390$ preserved in an explicit unscreened observation band.
  \end{itemize}
\item \textbf{Island-membership (survivor) collection:} Candidates occupying active island slots at generation end, including elite retention and periodic ring migration. In Cohort~1 (\text{Run~1}), $2{,}176$ slot occurrences are recorded across Islands~1--4 ($4\times 32$ slots over 17 generations), comprising $1{,}984$ initial placements, $192$ elite reappearances, and $12$ ring-migration events ($4$ events each at generations 4, 8, and 12).
\item \textbf{Quality-diversity archive:} Representatives retained per behavioral niche cell across each run.
\item \textbf{Independent Stage-2 / strict assessment collection:} The subset of top-ranked or separately exported candidates admitted to multi-gate CAD, plate, and quadratic solid verification (23 subjects in the historical battery plus the single assessed export Individual~2000 in the automatic certification leaderboard, 1 assessed candidate, 0 qualified, no promoted champion).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Recorded Multi-Hop Inheritance and Coordinate-Level Genome Diffs}
Figure~\ref{fig:lineage} traces the recorded three-hop ancestry of Individual~1095 (Cohort~1, \text{Run~1}, generation~9, highest stored birth fitness $\text{fit}=1.15727$) directly from the Cohort~1 evaluation ledger and 146-column population table (exact floating-point inequality $\Delta\neq 0$ across the 128 numeric gene columns $17..144$; raw-coordinate Euclidean $L_2$ norms mix millimeter, degree, and dimensionless units and are not physical geometric distances). Rather than a tree of independent ancestors, the $7$ retained individuals form a directed acyclic graph with $7$ three-hop parent edges and $1$ fourth-hop induced edge ($8$ induced edges total):
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Individual~174} (generation~1, $\text{fit}=1.14979$, archive crossover + mutation from parents $57$ and $61$) branches in the induced subgraph to produce both \textbf{Individual~245} (generation~2, $\text{fit}=1.15388$, archive mutation from sole parent $[174]$, $24/128$ changed numeric coordinates, raw-coordinate $L_2 = 0.604263$, $\max|\Delta|=0.361541$ across silhouette control points \code{cp01}--\code{cp26}) and, via the fourth-hop induced edge $174\to 299$, \textbf{Individual~299} (generation~2, $\text{fit}=1.14855$, archive mutation from sole parent $[174]$, $2/128$ changed coordinates: lattice depth $\Delta = -0.129784\,\mathrm{mm}$ and lattice pitch $\Delta = +0.108820\,\mathrm{mm}$, raw-coordinate $L_2 = 0.169369$).
\item \textbf{Individual~245} mutates into \textbf{Individual~413} (generation~3, $\text{fit}=1.15296$, archive mutation, sole recorded parent $[245]$, $5/128$ changed coordinates---comet terminal width, cairn depth, handle flute depth, surface texture depth, and texture angle $\Delta=+5.371762^\circ$---with raw-coordinate $L_2=5.385480$), while \textbf{Individual~299} mutates into \textbf{Individual~392} (generation~3, $\text{fit}=1.12512$, mutation, sole recorded parent $[299]$, $8/128$ changed coordinates, raw-coordinate $L_2=4.962176$, $\max|\Delta|=4.850458$). Individual~413 does \emph{not} descend from Individual~392.
\item \textbf{Individual~526} (generation~4, $\text{fit}=1.15563$, crossover + mutation, recorded parents $[413, 392]$) differs in $7/128$ numeric coordinates from parent~0 Individual~413 (raw-coordinate $L_2=0.724248$, $\max|\Delta|=0.648528$) and in $35/128$ coordinates from parent~1 Individual~392 (raw-coordinate $L_2=1.349493$, $\max|\Delta|=0.894505$).
\item \textbf{Individual~1095} (generation~9, $\text{fit}=1.15727$, crossover + mutation, recorded parents $[413, 526]$) reuses ancestor Individual~413 across two distinct paths ($413\to 1095$ and $413\to 526\to 1095$), differing in $13/128$ coordinates from parent~0 Individual~413 (raw-coordinate $L_2=0.560427$, $\max|\Delta|=0.277590$) and in $14/128$ coordinates from parent~1 Individual~526 (raw-coordinate $L_2=0.815184$, $\max|\Delta|=0.648528$; $11/128$ coordinates differ from both parents simultaneously, $3$ match only $413$, and $2$ match only $526$, while column~$145$ remains \code{Ti-6Al-4V} across all $8$ edges).
\end{itemize}
Crucially, when Individual~1095 was admitted to strict multi-model verification, it failed the regional failure-sequence requirement, demonstrating why high search-stage fitness and recorded lineage do not establish engineering qualification.
