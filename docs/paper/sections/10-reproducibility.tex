\section{Reproducibility and Publication Provenance}
\subsection{Four Different Reproduction Claims}
This manuscript reconstructs documentation projections from retained files; it does not rerun numerical models or validate physical specimens. The standalone Rust package reads the frozen battery and science catalogs, selected weighted summaries, historical births and admitted assessment records. Identity and consistency checks precede every output write. Chart reconstruction, solver replay, software-control execution and physical replication support different claims.

\subsection{Input and Output Identities}
The generated manifest records input/output SHA-256 identities. The studied baseline and publication revision remain distinct from original source-revision and freshness fields. The historical battery's differing member revision fields are preserved in Fig.~\ref{fig:battery-matrix}; hashes bind bytes without validating their physical meaning.

Receipts name field selectors and reductions: retained generation summaries for the weighted plot; historical \code{fit}, generation and status for population views; original parent lists for lineage; exact evaluator IDs for battery cells. Source pointers preserve nulls and omitted lineage boundaries. The science index retains all definitions regardless of favorable status.

The cover image is a separate presentation operation using Blender 4.5.3 Cycles, four CPU threads, 256 samples and seed 42. Its 3,600 by 2,800 pixel lossless master uses AgX and recorded denoising. The source STL is uniformly converted from millimetres to display metres; the object receives no geometry modifiers, remeshing or deformation. Original flat face normals and visible export faceting remain. The frozen input register and scene/render receipts record source hashes, camera, lighting, material recipe, color configuration and before/after imported vertex/face digests. The illustrative finish is not a computed field or evidence of manufacturing quality. These choices preserve the distinction between geometry identity, visual presentation and physical validation.

\subsection{Determinism and Check Mode}
Generation has no clock or network input. Read-only check mode recomputes projections and compares bytes. Invalid identity, membership, metric arithmetic, lineage chronology, typed flags or paths are refused before output writes. Valid numeric zero remains distinct from missing or Boolean data. Test receipts identify the exact local revision and fixture scope.

The separate PDF operation uses IEEEtran with a custom opening and records tool versions and source/output hashes under the lane-local target directory. It is distinct from the older pinned Pandoc build. Source and numerical projections remain inspectable alongside the rendered PDF; cross-toolchain byte identity is not claimed.

\subsection{Unavailable Sources}
The weighted cohort lacks its selected CAD geometry and referenced round-screen populations. Its summaries cannot supply a winner render or full population distribution. Missing wave bytes and missing solved spatial fields remain unavailable; a path or digest cannot reconstruct them. A reproducible chart therefore does not imply local replay of its original solver execution.

\subsection{Claim Register and Human Review}
Quantitative claims concern retained observations and their reporting consistency. Agent, manufacturing and scaling studies remain prospective. Author adversarial review is separate from independent reconstruction, scientific peer review and exact-head repository approval. Human authors must verify contributions, claims, references and the assistance disclosure before submission.

\subsection{How an Independent Reader Can Start}
The public build recipe reconstructs the documentation without a mechanics workload. Readers can compare hashes, inspect source selectors and audit atlas links before attempting separately scoped solver replay. Physical replication requires specimen identity, measurement methods and uncertainty. The artifact package's author receipts document completed reporting checks; they are not substitute solver or laboratory receipts.
