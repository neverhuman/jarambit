\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
This study followed one computational design result from its representation to its evidence. A versioned representation and decoder, recorded evolutionary search and a declared multi-objective score produced \AllRunsLeader{}, the highest of \num{\AllRunsStageTwo} comparable Stage-2 assessments at \AllRunsLeaderScore{} of 100, and a complete re-assessment gave the same value.\ifdefined\JRSmoothedVariant{} Surface-tension pocket smoothing then replaced its chipped pocket walls with clean contours inside the CAD export, and the full assessment of the new solid, not its appearance, gave \PocketSmoothedScore{}.\fi{} At every step the retained links say which stage, which scorer and which export a number belongs to.

Three lessons come from the records rather than from the design. \emph{A ranking is a declared preference.} \code{W_ULTRALIGHT_v1} ranks every candidate continuously, and the design on the first page passes \ifdefined\JRSmoothedVariant\PocketBatteryAfter\else\PocketBatteryBefore\fi{} of 18 battery evaluators and is not qualified. \emph{A comparison is only as good as its scorer.} In the four re-assessed cases, stored values from an early evaluator exceeded the canonical score of the same genome by \AllRunsInflationRange{} points; only recomputation from retained scorecards made the census comparable. \emph{A change of geometry is a new design.}\ifdefined\JRSmoothedVariant{} One smoothing setting raised the score of the design it was chosen on and lowered that of the current best, so it is assessed design by design.\else{} Any post-process of the export, however cosmetic, needs its own assessment.\fi

What the study does not establish is equally specific. It reports no physical observation, no qualification, no comparison with other optimizers or with expert designs, and no isolated benefit of any feature. Agreement between two solvers on a shared mesh checks implementation, not the model, and the plate mesh study refuses its extrapolated error estimates.

The arrangement is not specific to a knife. The search consumes scores; the representation, the decoder and the tests carry the domain. Where a design can be written in an executable representation and its consequential behaviour can be measured by tests that remain credible where a search will go, the same loop can propose and rank candidates for fixtures in aerospace and spaceflight hardware, components for medical-device research, grippers and compliant parts in robotics, passages for thermal management, mounts for scientific instruments and parts of energy systems. Each domain adds its own obligations before a computed preference can become an accepted part: validated models, dimensional metrology and process characterization for the manufacturing route, and physical tests at the conditions of use (Table~\ref{tab:prospective}). Additive manufacturing widens the set of geometries that can be made; it does not by itself supply precision, fatigue performance or qualification.

\begin{eibox}{The claim, stated conditionally}
Given a representation that can express useful designs, an objective that says what is preferred, and tests that are credible where the search goes, computational search can explore designs and trade-offs that are impractical to enumerate by hand, and it can leave an evidence trail that another researcher can follow. This paper demonstrates that trail for one computational case. Proof for any built object remains specific to that object and its intended use.
\end{eibox}
