\section*{Data and Code Availability}
All source, retained evidence, receipts and renders are public at \href{https://github.com/neverhuman/jarambit}{\code{github.com/neverhuman/jarambit}}. Every generated number in the paper comes from \code{crates/paper-artifacts} and a named receipt; the Supplement maps each figure and table to its inputs and hashes. Rebuilding the paper reconstructs reports only; it runs no new search, CAD export or solve.
