\section{Surface-Tension Pocket Smoothing}
\label{sec:pocket-smoothing}

\textit{Why the walls were chipped.} The exported solid is a height field over a fixed planform: faces $z=\pm t(x,y)/2$, with side walls on the outline and on every through-hole. Every weight-saving feature (isogrid pockets, lattice dimples, fullers, flutes, relief) is a removal depth $D=t_{\mathrm{base}}-t$. Before this release that depth reached the CAD through the $0.25\,\mathrm{mm}$ thickness raster by nearest-cell lookup, so most pocket walls were vertical steps along a staircase. A constrained Delaunay triangulation (CDT) of about $0.7\,\mathrm{mm}$ triangles sampled those steps; its triangles cut across them, and the result renders as chipped, crystalline walls (Figure~\ref{fig:pocket-bw}, left). The mesh smoothing of the smoothing ladder (\code{docs/paper/smoothing-ladder.pdf}) cannot remove this. It pins every vertex on an edge sharper than $30^\circ$, which is exactly where the chips are, and its largest displacement at the declared setting is $0.214\,\mathrm{mm}$.

\textit{Method.} We smooth the removal depth itself, before meshing, as a liquid would. Each pocket is a basin: a connected component of $D>0.02\,\mathrm{mm}$ (a watershed), merged across gaps narrower than $r/2$. Inside its basin the depth evolves for time $T=r^2/2$ by level-set curvature motion with a per-basin pressure,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:pocket-flow}
D_t=|\nabla D|\,\nabla\!\cdot\!\frac{\nabla D}{|\nabla D|}+\lambda_b\,|\nabla D|,
\end{equation}
where $\lambda_b$ keeps the removed volume of basin $b$ constant (the volume of oil in the pit). Every depth contour then shortens like a two-dimensional droplet of fixed area under line tension:
\begin{itemize}
\item chips and spikes, which have the highest curvature, go first, and a chipped circle becomes a circle;
\item straight rib edges stay straight, and corners round to a radius of about $r$;
\item walls keep their steepness, so ribs keep their width.
\end{itemize}
No basin may end deeper than its own deepest original point, so the volume constraint widens a thin groove instead of drilling it toward the minimum web. The planform, through-holes, grind faces, bore collar, raised features, brand pad and a $1\,\mathrm{mm}$ rim of unpocketed material along the outline and holes never move.

The flow runs on a $0.125\,\mathrm{mm}$ grid with explicit steps of $0.2h^2$ and upwind gradients for the pressure term; it takes about 3\,s for this design. The exporter then reads $t=t_{\mathrm{base}}-D$ by bilinear interpolation. It adds CDT vertices in three rows along every wall (depth contours at 8, 50 and 92\,\% of the local wall height) and in pairs straddling the grind boundary, so triangle edges follow each wall instead of cutting across it. Equation~(\ref{eq:pocket-flow}) is the $a=0$ member of a family whose $a=1$ member is graph mean-curvature flow of a liquid film; we compare both below. The switch is \code{JARAMBIT_POCKET_SMOOTH} (\code{crates/crescent/src/pocket_smooth.rs}); \code{on} selects $r=0.75\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $a=0$. It is off by default, and with it unset every export is byte-identical to the previous release. An enabled setting is part of the certificate identity, so smoothed and as-exported evidence never share a key.

\textit{Settings and fitness.} We exported the design at $r=0.5$, $0.75$, $1.0$, $1.5$ and $2.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ with $a=0$, and at $r=0.75\,\mathrm{mm}$ with $a=1$. Every export was re-assessed with the complete Stage-2 evaluator. Unlike the mesh smoothing of variant~B, nothing is withheld: the smoothed design is an ordinary export, so its STEP solid, 3D mass, plate responses, solid-tip submodel and every other objective are measured on the smoothed geometry. Table~\ref{tab:pocket-score} gives the results together with an unsmoothed re-run of the same evaluator, which reproduces the retained $80.299390$ exactly.

\textit{Results.} Smoothing leaves the design's engineering responses essentially where they were.
\begin{itemize}
\item The removed volume of every basin is preserved to rounding, and the exported volume changes by $-3.19\,\mathrm{mm^3}$ ($-0.017\,\%$) at $r=0.75\,\mathrm{mm}$.
\item Mass stays at $83.19$--$83.26\,\mathrm{g}$, against $83.2231\,\mathrm{g}$ as exported.
\item The lateral capacity rises by $0.2$--$0.8\,\mathrm{N}$, and the ring, body-twist and fatigue margins move by less than $3\,\%$.
\item The failure-sequence ratio rises from $1.29$ to $1.45$--$1.62$, and the CAD/B-rep objective keeps its five points in every case.
\end{itemize}

What moves $F$ is the 3D solid-tip submodel. Its nodal-maximum safety factor changes non-monotonically with $r$: $0.6353$, $0.7024$, $0.6811$ and $0.6275$ for $r=0.5$ to $1.5\,\mathrm{mm}$, against $0.6823$ as exported. At $r=2.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ the tip solve produced no observation, which the evaluator scores as zero. We read these changes as the sensitivity of a nodal maximum near the fuller end to the local triangulation, not as a structural trend; the two solvers agree within $0.08\,\%$ in every measured case. The setting $r=0.75\,\mathrm{mm}$ sits between the two we preferred visually, and it is also the best measured: $F=80.370405$, $0.071015$ above the as-exported design. It is the design shown on the first page (Figure~\ref{fig:winner-cover}) and in Figure~\ref{fig:cover}.

Larger $r$ makes pockets rounder and more alike. At $r=2.0\,\mathrm{mm}$ small pockets merge into rounded blobs, and the film variant ($a=1$) spreads depth sideways into the ribs; neither improves $F$. The smoothing has a cost in export size: the chosen export has about 40\,\% more triangles than the raw one (STL $21.2$ against $15.3\,\mathrm{MB}$, STEP $86.7$ against $53.4\,\mathrm{MB}$). Its tip submodel has $177{,}984$ tetrahedra against $91{,}008$, and a full Stage-2 assessment took about three hours instead of about 35 minutes, mostly in STEP verification and the larger tip solve.

\textit{Scope.} The smoothing changes interior pocket geometry only. It adds no physical solve beyond the Stage-2 evaluator, no qualification and no physical observation. The Individual~2000 plate fields of Section~\ref{sec:fitness} are retained evidence of a different design and are not smoothed. Receipts, scorecards and every exploratory setting are in \code{experiments/pocket-surface-tension-smoothing/}.
