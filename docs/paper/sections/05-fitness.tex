\section{Fitness Profiles, Priority Groupings, and Stage Semantics}
\label{sec:fitness}

\subsection{Three Distinct Score Regimes and Forbidden Cross-Profile Splicing}
Because different search cohorts and assessment stages evaluate different mathematical functions, a common word such as ``fitness'' does not establish comparability across runs. Jarambit's score registry enforces strict separation across three profile families, prohibiting cross-profile splicing:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Native Quality-Diversity Search Fitness ($f$, Cohort~1 \text{Run~1} and Cohort~3 \text{Run~2}--\text{Run~11}):} In the four-island crescent search engine, native fitness $f$ (higher is better) applies conditional legality and feasibility branches: geometrically illegal or unmeshed candidates receive unbounded negative penalty scores ($f < 0$, down to $-207.23$ in Cohort~1), whereas legal candidates combine normalized mechanical/mass terms with archive-contextual novelty computed against the contemporaneous population and archive. Because novelty is re-evaluated as the archive evolves, $f$ is a contextual search observation rather than an intrinsic physical constant of a genome.
\item \textbf{Retained Weighted Reporting Profile ($F \in [0,100]$, Cohort~2 Reporting Curves):} In the multi-stage ultralight reporting profile, twenty normalized component terms $s_i \in [0,1]$ are combined with percentage weights summing to $100$:
\begin{equation}
 F = \sum_{i=1}^{20} w_i s_i,\qquad 0\le s_i \le 1,\quad \sum_{i=1}^{20} w_i = 100.
\end{equation}
The twenty terms are organized into four priority groups:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Mass and compactness ($30\%$):} ultralight mass and holder/envelope compactness.
  \item \emph{Form and comfort proxies ($25\%$):} karambit ring/claw archetype, silhouette similarity, and bore-collar geometry proxy.
  \item \emph{Exported geometry ($15\%$):} watertight single-solid B-rep topology, exported edge sharpness, and point profile.
  \item \emph{Response and verification ($30\%$):} lateral loads ($180\,\mathrm{N}$ and $260\,\mathrm{N}$ coarse and refined), ring traction ($1500\,\mathrm{N}$), body and local tip torsion ($1.82\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$), quadratic solid tip response and cross-solver agreement, elastic support/buckling, regional yield-order sequence ($\ge 1.2$), three-level plate mesh study, human-maximum overload, and Goodman fatigue screen.
  \end{itemize}
Within the six retained Cohort~2 reporting curves (15 generation summaries), Individual~2002 records the highest retained weighted reporting profile value ($F = 74.207299$, mass $101.139\,\mathrm{g}$ raster / $101.138\,\mathrm{g}$ solid). Several terms in $F$ are clipped ratios, Boolean indicators, or screening proxies; a higher value of $F$ orders candidates for research attention without establishing qualification.
\item \textbf{Shortfall-Penalized CAD Score ($W_{\text{CAD}}$) and Global Composite ($G_1$):} In 16-gate CAD evaluation, candidates satisfying all 16 legacy engineering gates receive a positive score in $[1.0,2.0]$ weighted by mass ($0.70$), archetype ($0.12$), survival buffer ($0.10$), sequence buffer ($0.05$), and carry stability ($0.03$):
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 W_{\text{CAD}}^{\text{pass}} &= 1.0 + 0.70\,s_{\text{mass}} + 0.12\,s_{\text{karambit}} \\
 &\quad + 0.10\,s_{\text{survival}} + 0.05\,s_{\text{seq}} + 0.03\,s_{\text{carry}},
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
whereas candidates failing any gate receive a negative shortfall penalty:
\begin{equation}
 W_{\text{CAD}}^{\text{fail}} = -\left(1.0 + 4.0\sum_{k\in\text{shortfalls}}\Delta_k\right) + 0.10\,s_{\text{mass}} < 0.
\end{equation}
Alongside $W_{\text{CAD}}$, the dimensionless composite score $G_1$ (higher is better, with no physical millimeter unit and no $0.45$ cutoff gate) evaluates:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 G_1 &= 0.35\,\min(\mathrm{SF}_{\text{solid\_tip}},\,2.0) + 0.35\,\frac{\mathrm{SPG}}{4.0} \\
 &\quad + 0.20\,s_{\text{aes}} - 0.05\,\frac{\max(0,\,m_g - 77.0)}{10.0} \\
 &\quad - 0.05\,\frac{\max(0,\,1.2 - r_{\text{seq}})}{1.2}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
For the separately assessed controlled export Individual~2001, $W_{\text{CAD}} = -3.878704$, $G_1 = 0.623903$, and mass $= 75.382\,\mathrm{g}$ ($75.1992\,\mathrm{g}$ 3D solid), meeting $11$ of $16$ legacy gates and failing $5$; for companion controlled export Individual~2007, $W_{\text{CAD}} = -3.964924$, $G_1 = 0.621634$, and mass $= 75.642\,\mathrm{g}$ ($75.5049\,\mathrm{g}$ 3D solid), likewise meeting $11$ of $16$ legacy gates and failing the same $5$ gates while failing grouped verification.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Reversible Display-Loss Conventions}
To support minimization-oriented progress plots without altering underlying stored evidence, each profile defines an explicit order-preserving display loss $L$:
\begin{equation}
 L_{\text{native}} = -f,\qquad L_{\text{ultralight}} = 100 - F,\qquad L_{\text{CAD}} = -W_{\text{CAD}}.
\end{equation}
For cumulative best-so-far series $H_g = \max_{0\le k\le g} r_k$, the corresponding display-loss envelope $-H_g$ or $100 - H_g$ is monotone non-increasing by construction. All figures declare both the display-loss transform and the native score values, preserving negative penalty tails without clipping.

\subsection{Retained Spatial Plate Fields and Saliency Crops for Individual 2000}
The retained $0.25\,\mathrm{mm}^2$ plate artifact for Individual~2000 ($13{,}503$ nodes, $25{,}209$ triangles) stores seven per-triangle arrays: five structural demand maps---the scaled multi-case von Mises envelope ($\mathrm{MPa}$, maximum $1{,}388.6695\,\mathrm{MPa}$, with $75$ cells inside the $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ tip exclusion forced to zero by the producer and rendered in neutral gray), unit out-of-plane lateral bending response ($\mathrm{MPa/N}$, $25{,}209$ active cells), unit in-plane tip-pry T6 membrane response ($\mathrm{MPa/N}$, $25{,}209$ active cells; in the multi-case envelope construction, both lateral and pry unit responses are multiplied by $260\,\mathrm{N}$), unit ring-traction T6 centroid response ($\mathrm{MPa/N}$, $10{,}334$ active cells and $14{,}875$ seated-region masked cells, scaled by $1500\,\mathrm{N}$ in the envelope), and unit chord-torsion response ($\mathrm{MPa/(N\,mm)}$, $25{,}209$ active cells, scaled by $1820\,\mathrm{N\,mm}$ in the envelope)---together with two auxiliary fields: per-element safety factor (dimensionless) and local element thickness ($\mathrm{mm}$). Figure~\ref{fig:plate-fields} displays the retained demand maps, while Figures~\ref{fig:hero-composite} and~\ref{fig:saliency-crops} pair the unlit 2D 5-map percentile composite index (combining the multi-case envelope and unit lateral, pry, ring-traction, and torsion maps, with contributor support $q(x)\in\{3/5,4/5,5/5\}$) and $\ge 90\%$ integrated-saliency local crops with whole-object picture-in-picture locators.
