\section{Score Definitions and Comparability}
\label{sec:fitness}
A search preference, a stored reporting vector, a shortfall score and a mechanical safety factor answer different questions. Their names, directions, observation stages and missing-input rules must accompany their values. Table~\ref{tab:score-dictionary} keeps the score regimes separate; a shared word such as ``fitness'' does not create a common performance axis.

\subsection{A Retained Weighted Reporting Vector}
The principal reporting definition is
\begin{equation}
 F=\sum_{i=1}^{20}w_i s_i,\qquad s_i\in[0,1],\quad \sum_iw_i=100.
\end{equation}
Its priorities are mass/compactness (30\%), form/comfort proxies (25\%), exported geometry (15\%) and response/verification (30\%). Supplement Table~\ref{S-tab:weighted-priorities} shows sums of \emph{stored weighted contributions} for Individual~2002. The Supplement retains all twenty normalized values, weights and contributions. These are reported observations from its summary, not a recalculation under the present scorer and not measurements transferred from Individual~2000.

The saved scalar is $F=\BestFitness{}$, with reported raster mass $101.139\,\mathrm{g}$ and solid-mass scalar $101.1384\,\mathrm{g}$. Its exact genome and certificate link to a retained proposal and screen; a separate leaderboard retains a later-assessment summary with \code{qualified=false}. The underlying exported geometry and full scorecard files are not retained at its referenced paths in the checked repository tree. The screen's earlier ``pending independent top-k assessment'' label is not that later summary's outcome. Individual~2000 supplies the illustrated assessed geometry and response fields.

A versioned twenty-term recipe is recoverable, but its exact historical execution and the curves' point stages remain unresolved. Several saved inputs match that recipe's hard-coded fallbacks for bore radius, edge/point profiles and overload proxies. The four whole terms containing those inputs contribute $15.682353$ points; this is not a claim that every point in those terms is an imputed measurement. Some absent-key Boolean branches also receive favorable defaults. The Supplement distinguishes these default-compatible inputs and historical bookkeeping from new reporting-layer imputation. The original vector is preserved, without rescoring.

For the assessed Individual~2000, the actual failed-branch score is $W_{\mathrm{CAD}}=-5.570408$. The same scorecard stores unpenalized quality $1.456983$; that is the hypothetical pass-branch value, not an additional achieved score. The Supplement gives the branch equations, input names and shortfall arithmetic. Those scores and $G_1$ remain separate from $F$ and native $f$.

\subsection{Context and Display Direction}
Native fitness includes novelty computed against contemporaneous population/archive descriptors. A value can therefore change when its context changes, even if the genome does not. Birth scores and later membership observations also have distinct stages. Cross-generation or cross-run maxima describe retained contextual preference; they do not isolate a physical improvement. The historical and newer native implementations/configurations require separate provenance even when both call their scalar $f$.

A display loss $L=-f$, $100-F$, or $-W_{\mathrm{CAD}}$ reverses numerical order. Minimizing it preserves the original preference for a larger native score. It does not make those different profiles comparable. A cumulative maximum is monotone by construction, so its corresponding loss is nonincreasing by construction; that fact alone is not evidence of continuing physical improvement.

The missing-aware response display (the roster of retained channels, the per-cell percentile index and its coverage) is defined in Supplement Section~\ref{S-sec:moved-display}.

Figure~\ref{fig:plate-fields} gives source-coordinate views of the envelope and three physical-unit responses; the fourth eligible response, in-plane tip pry, appears in Supplement Figure~\ref{S-fig:saliency-crops}C. An upper-value cutoff is a fraction of the original eligible value range, not a count or area quantile. Gray denotes producer-masked cells, not measured zero stress.
