\subsection{Cohort 3: Ten-Run 800-Generation Population Census (\text{Run 2}--\text{Run 11})}
Figure~\ref{fig:population-latest} summarizes the ten retained four-island quality-diversity runs that completed generation~800 (\text{Run~2} through \text{Run~11}) alongside the separate historical (\text{Run~1}) and intermediate screening regimes ($1{,}954{,}560$ native + $1{,}984$ historical + $2{,}259$ screened $= 1{,}958{,}803$ numeric scored observations plus $1{,}390$ unscreened proposals in a separate band $= 1{,}960{,}193$ admitted records across separate search regimes, $0$ excluded). Each Cohort~3 run evaluated $195{,}456$ births across $801$ generations ($0$--$800$) and $3{,}204$ generation-by-island summary rows ($4$ islands $\times 801$ generations), yielding $1{,}954{,}560$ complete 146-column birth records and $32{,}040$ summary rows in total. Across the ten retained runs, $754{,}236$ evaluations ($38.59\%$) satisfy search-level geometric legality ($610{,}051$ with native $f < 0$ due to soft/novelty terms and $144{,}185$ with $f \ge 0$, retaining the $-10.0 \le f < -2.0$ band), with per-run legal counts ranging from $70{,}278$ ($35.96\%$, \text{Run~6}) to $80{,}132$ ($41.00\%$, \text{Run~11}), a sample mean of $75{,}423.6$, a sample standard deviation ($s$, $\mathrm{ddof}=1$) of $2{,}936.55$ (population SD $\sigma=2{,}785.86$), and a median of $75{,}579.0$ ($\mathrm{IQR}=2{,}908.75$). In Figure~\ref{fig:population-latest}, Panels~A1 ($802\times 440$ full $\operatorname{asinh}(f)$ grid over $[-215.0, +1.20]$) and~A2 ($802\times 260$ bounded linear detail over $[-0.60, +1.16]$ with $727{,}077$ in-window points and $1{,}227{,}483$ conserved in the bottom tail strip) encode occupied generation-by-score bins using logarithmic count shading and per-bin majority legality color ($\text{legal}\ge\text{penalty}$); this count aggregation hides minority classifications where legal and penalty rows share a bin and does not render every point separately or equate score sign with legality. At generation~$0$, five of the ten runs have non-null legal-population medians (\text{Run~2}: $-0.2269$, \text{Run~4}: $-0.2070$, \text{Run~6}: $-0.25095$, \text{Run~8}: $-0.2244$, \text{Run~11}: $-0.2620$; range $-0.2620$ to $-0.2070$) while the remaining five runs have null legal medians at generation~$0$; across all $801$ generations and all ten runs, the minimum legal median is $-0.5782$ (\text{Run~9}, generation~$3$), and at generation~$800$ all ten legal-population medians remain negative, ranging from $-0.2610$ (\text{Run~11}) to $-0.1933$ (\text{Run~4}) (mean $-0.2272$).

Per-birth Stage-2 CAD assessment records are absent for the $1{,}954{,}560$ native search rows in Cohort~3, establishing missing retained assessments for those births.

\subsection{Cohort 1: Historical 17-Generation Genealogy and Stage Reversal (\text{Run 1})}
In Cohort~1 (\text{Run~1}), all \BirthCount\ retained births across generations $0$--$16$ are preserved with exact parent links, island assignments, and finite-element evaluation status (\FemComplete\ FEM-complete records, \FemNotEvaluated\ not-evaluated records, and $446$ search-legal births; Figure~\ref{fig:population}). Generation~0 contains $63$ unevaluated records out of $128$, whereas generation~16 contains $21$ out of $116$, illustrating why pooled score distributions shift as legality-filter pass rates change.

The highest stored birth score in Cohort~1 is \HistoricalBestFit\ for Individual~\HistoricalBestId\ in generation~\HistoricalBestGeneration\ (display loss $-1.15727$, raw plate envelope maximum $685.94\,\mathrm{MPa}$). Joining Individual~\HistoricalBestId\ to the retained strict-assessment summary shows that strict verification was not met (\HistoricalBestAllPass, failing the regional yield-order sequence threshold). Thus, in this retained historical cohort pairing complete multi-hop genealogy with subsequent strict assessment, the retained historical top-ranked birth failed its later strict assessment.

\subsection{Cohort 2: Intermediate Multi-Stage Screening and Retained Weighted Reporting Profile}
Across 24 multi-stage screening run directories, Cohort~2 retains $3{,}649$ birth proposals across $57$ round-birth files. Validated one-based candidate indexing joins $2{,}259$ proposals to Stage-1 screening observations, while $1{,}390$ proposals remain in an explicit unscreened observation band (and a zero-based index negative control is verified to reject $2{,}247$ misjoined pairs).

Within the retained weighted reporting profile subset of Cohort~2, six admitted reporting curve files retain fifteen generation summaries (\text{Run~31} through \text{Run~36}, displayed in Figure~\ref{fig:weighted}). Four of the five multi-row series retain their initial archive value across subsequent rounds. In \text{Run~31}, the stored archive advances from $74.167488$ (Individual~2009 at generation~$0$) to \BestFitness\ (Individual~2002 at generations~$1$--$2$, display loss $25.792701$, $13$ favorable legacy comparisons out of $26$ overlapping predicates/groups, not qualified), while the third round's best is $73.608446$. Because the underlying round-population files and exported CAD geometry for Individual~2002 are absent from the retained directories, these short reporting curves are presented without inventing population uncertainty bands or substituting another cohort's geometry.

\subsection{Separately Assessed Controlled Exports, Automatic Leaderboard, and Battery Reconciliation}
In two separately assessed controlled-probe exports (distinct from the evolutionary birth cloud of \text{Run~1}), candidate records were evaluated under the 16-gate CAD scorecard:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Individual~2001:} Records $W_{\text{CAD}}=-3.878704$, $G_1=0.6239029999999999$, raster mass $75.382\,\mathrm{g}$ (3D solid mass $75.1992\,\mathrm{g}$), and passes $11$ of $16$ legacy gates while failing $5$ (certification and refined $260\,\mathrm{N}$ lateral bending, tip-first yield-order ratio $\ge 1.2$, $1.82\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ local plate tip torsion, and $1.82\,\mathrm{N\cdot m}$ quadratic solid tip submodel).
\item \textbf{Individual~2007:} Records $W_{\text{CAD}}=-3.964924$, $G_1=0.6216340416666666$, raster mass $75.642\,\mathrm{g}$ (3D solid mass $75.5049\,\mathrm{g}$), and likewise passes $11$ of $16$ legacy gates while failing the same $5$ gates.
\end{itemize}
Both controlled exports failed grouped strict requirement verification.

In the fixed 23-subject historical CAD battery ($23\times 18 = 414$ cells; preserved in the linked Supplement \nolinkurl{generated/battery-matrix.svg} and Section~\ref{sec:reproducibility}), \BatteryPass\ stored passes, \BatteryFail\ failures, and \BatteryBlocked\ blocked records are reconciled, with $92$ passes attributable to four shared software-control columns and $20$ passes in the quadratic-solid tip column recording file completeness rather than favorable strength. Separately, the automatic certification leaderboard contains a single assessed export (Individual~2000; 1 assessed candidate, 0 qualified, no promoted champion), which is not qualified.

\subsection{Research Questions Answered and Open}
Our empirical findings answer the study's central research question through bounded, reproducible evidence: (1)~the 129-field executable genome and island variation operators generate inspectable gene-coordinate records and recorded population changes across $1{,}954{,}560$ evaluated births in Cohort~3 and $1{,}984$ genealogically linked births in Cohort~1; (2)~the retained historical top-ranked birth (Individual~1095 in Cohort~1) failed its later strict assessment, and separately assessed exports (Individual~2000, Individual~2001, Individual~2002, and Individual~2007) likewise fail strict multi-gate qualification; and (3)~matched-mesh quadratic solid solvers agree within $0.05\%$ while three-level plate GCI estimates must be refused when observed orders are negative or oscillatory. Measuring formal structural diversity, conducting representation ablations, fabricating physical specimens, and performing cyclic fatigue testing remain open prospective work.
