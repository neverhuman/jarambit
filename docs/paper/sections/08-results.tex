\section{Retrospective Results and What They Establish}
\subsection{Corpus Reconciliation}
The historical battery manifest names 23 subject files and 18 evaluator IDs. The publication reader checks that every subject contains exactly those IDs, that no identity is duplicated, and that the stored statuses belong to the declared vocabulary. The resulting 414 cells reconcile to the distribution in Table~\ref{tab:battery}. This is a deterministic reporting result: it demonstrates what the retained corpus contains, not a new mechanics experiment.

The distinction between unfavorable and blocked is preserved. The combined requirement column contains nineteen unfavorable and four blocked records. The three geometry-binding/unit/topology columns are blocked for all subjects. Four shared software/capability columns repeat favorable status across the corpus. The local-solid column records completeness for twenty entries. Together, these observations explain why a total favorable-cell percentage would be misleading as a physical engineering summary.

The separate automatic proof has one completed assessment, zero qualified designs and a null champion field. It remains separate from the fixed historical battery. The weighted cohort is another retained set with its own membership. Adding these denominators would produce a repository-wide total with no clear sampling or assessment meaning.

\subsection{The Selected Weighted Cohort}
The retained w411c3 reporting profile identifies \code{w411c3_r02_p10_c12} at fitness \BestFitness. Its stored legacy tally is thirteen favorable comparisons out of 26 overlapping predicates/groups. The cohort has no qualified design. The score is the best reported profile in this selected cohort, not a proven global optimum, not an all-measured native selection value, and not a physical-performance certificate.

The three retained summaries show a best-so-far score of 74.167488, then 74.207299, then the same 74.207299. The third round's reported best is lower, at 73.608446, while the archive remains unchanged. These values reconcile as a stored recurrence. Incomplete stage lineage prevents treating that arithmetic agreement as evidence of progress under one fixed objective.

The best-record CAD geometry is absent from this retained cohort. Accordingly, the opening figure is a score profile, not an invented render of the candidate. The referenced round files are also unavailable here, preventing reconstruction of a full weighted birth distribution. These absences are part of the result. Supplying a different geometry or a synthetic cloud would make the figure more visually complete while making its scientific claim less accurate.

\subsection{Historical Population Behavior}
The w282 source contains \BirthCount\ retained births. Figure~\ref{fig:population} separates \FemComplete\ FEM-complete records from \FemNotEvaluated\ not-evaluated cases. Generation 0 contains 63 not-evaluated records out of 128; generation 16 contains 21 out of 116. Changing evaluation-status composition therefore contributes to the pooled distribution. The generated summary retains both pooled statistics and FEM-complete medians, plus per-generation status/legality counts, without imputing results for unsolved cases.

The highest stored score is \HistoricalBestFit\ for ID \HistoricalBestId\ in generation \HistoricalBestGeneration. Joining that ID and generation to the retained strict-assessment summary gives \texttt{cert\_pass\_all}=\HistoricalBestAllPass. A favorable search ranking did not imply passing the subsequent assessment. This is a concrete consequence of separating search observations from assessment findings, rather than merely a general warning about fitness.

The original \href{https://github.com/neverhuman/jarambit/blob/009f5ac520fa3afe8ed8025230eef604dbb7f922/evidence/rs_v2/w282_ultrarunner/search_summary.json}{selection summary} states that GA strict-certified passers did not beat its baseline; two controlled probes supplied the named exports that passed its historical 16-predicate comparison. Those producer statements retain their pre-correction model scope and do not establish current qualification. The controlled probes are distinct from the best scored member of the retained evolutionary population. Their geometry must not be paired with this curve as if it depicted one best individual's trajectory.

The dataset does not establish that every proposed birth was logged, that survivors have the same distribution, or that its contextual fitness is comparable with later weighted scores. It does support inspection of actual search behavior and its subsequent assessment outcome. No missing newer population or field is reconstructed from these historical observations.

\subsection{Corrections as Engineering Evidence}
The repository preserves withdrawn certification claims and corrected records. Those records should remain visible because they show the importance of geometry identity, independent solver execution, field semantics and claim scope. A previously favorable historical screen cannot be promoted into a current qualified result merely because its number is attractive. Nor should a correction be described as a fresh solve unless new execution evidence actually exists.

This paper does not independently replay the historical correction campaign. It uses the retained notices and corrected artifacts to explain their reporting implications. The observation is methodological: scientific credibility requires the ability to revise a public conclusion while preserving the original assertion, the reason for correction and the identity of the new supporting evidence.

\subsection{Research Questions Answered and Open}
The first research question is answered descriptively: candidate meaning depends on representation, decoder, export and analysis identities, not just a vector or filename. The second is answered by the complete diagnostic/evaluator taxonomy and the recomputed fixed-corpus counts. The third remains a research agenda: no agent-representation ablation, compute-scaling experiment, manufacturing-tolerance study or physical qualification is supplied here.

These limits do not erase the engineering depth of the existing platform. They identify where that depth is implemented, where observations are retained, where findings are unfavorable, and where stronger claims need new evidence. A serious research paper should make those distinctions easy to inspect rather than treating them as objections to be hidden from the reader.
