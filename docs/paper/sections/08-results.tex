\section{Retrospective Results and What They Establish}
\subsection{Corpus Reconciliation}
The historical battery contains 23 subject files, 18 exact evaluator IDs and 414 cells, reconciled in Fig.~\ref{fig:battery-matrix}. The reader checks membership, duplicate identities and status vocabulary; its ledger preserves nullable evidence paths, original kinds and differing revision fields. These are retained reporting observations, not new mechanics experiments.

The combined requirement column has nineteen unfavorable and four blocked records. Three geometry-binding/unit/topology columns are blocked for every subject. Four shared software columns repeat favorable references, while the local-solid column records completeness for twenty entries. A pooled favorable-cell percentage would therefore misrepresent physical evidence. The separate automatic proof has one completed assessment, zero qualified designs and a null champion. Its denominator is kept separate from the historical battery and weighted summaries.

\subsection{Six Retained Weighted Reporting Series}
Six admitted \code{fitness_curve.json} files contain fifteen saved generation summaries: \code{w501_ul}, \code{w409_ul}, \code{w411c3} and \code{w411t3} have three each; \code{w322} has two; \code{w501_xb1} has one. Figure~\ref{fig:weighted} shows every stored round-best and Hall-of-Fame observation, grouped by source directory/run. These directory labels do not establish independent experimental replications. All fifteen named round files are absent from the admitted record directories, so these summaries cannot supply birth distributions, medians or uncertainty bands.

Four of the five files with multiple rows retain their first reported archive value throughout the saved summaries. Later round-best values can fall while that archive stays fixed. The single \code{w501_xb1} summary supports one observation pair, not a trajectory. In \code{w411c3}, the saved archive changes from 74.167488 to 74.207299 and then remains there; the third round's best is 73.608446. These are retained reporting outcomes. Their common \code{W_ULTRALIGHT_v1} name does not establish identical stage/scorer inputs, and no pooled convergence curve or compute-scaling effect is inferred.

The selected w411c3 profile identifies \code{w411c3_r02_p10_c12} at \BestFitness, with thirteen favorable legacy comparisons out of 26 overlapping predicates/groups and no qualified design. Its detailed component profile remains in the supplementary generated artifacts. It is the best reported profile within that selected cohort, not an all-measured native selection result or a physical-performance certificate. The publication preserves unresolved producer/scorer/stage fields as unknown rather than assigning a revision from the metric name.

The weighted best-record CAD geometry is unavailable in the selected cohort. The cover instead depicts the separately identified assessed example \code{cert_pipeline_r01_p14_c02}. It is neither that weighted best design nor the historical population's best individual. No missing geometry or individual population is reconstructed from the short summaries.

\subsection{Historical Population Behavior}
The w282 source contains \BirthCount\ retained births. Figure~\ref{fig:population} separates \FemComplete\ FEM-complete records from \FemNotEvaluated\ not-evaluated cases. Generation 0 contains 63 not-evaluated records out of 128; generation 16 contains 21 out of 116. Changing evaluation-status composition therefore contributes to the pooled distribution. The generated summary retains both pooled statistics and FEM-complete medians, plus per-generation status/legality counts, without imputing results for unsolved cases.

The highest stored score is \HistoricalBestFit\ for ID \HistoricalBestId\ in generation \HistoricalBestGeneration. Joining that ID and generation to the retained strict-assessment summary gives \texttt{cert\_pass\_all}=\HistoricalBestAllPass. A favorable search ranking did not imply passing the subsequent assessment. This is a concrete consequence of separating search observations from assessment findings, rather than merely a general warning about fitness.

The original \href{https://github.com/neverhuman/jarambit/blob/009f5ac520fa3afe8ed8025230eef604dbb7f922/evidence/rs_v2/w282_ultrarunner/search_summary.json}{selection summary} states that GA strict-certified passers did not beat its baseline; two controlled probes supplied the named exports that passed its historical 16-predicate comparison. Those producer statements retain their pre-correction model scope and do not establish current qualification. The controlled probes are distinct from the best scored member of the retained evolutionary population. Their geometry must not be paired with this curve as if it depicted one best individual's trajectory.

The dataset does not establish that every proposed birth was logged, that survivors have the same distribution, or that its contextual fitness is comparable with later weighted scores. It does support inspection of actual search behavior and its subsequent assessment outcome. No missing newer population or field is reconstructed from these historical observations.

\subsection{Corrections as Engineering Evidence}
The repository preserves withdrawn certification claims, notices and corrected records. A favorable historical screen cannot establish current qualification, and a correction is not a fresh solve without new execution evidence. This retrospective study does not replay that correction campaign. The retained material instead demonstrates why a revised conclusion needs the original assertion, reason for correction and identity of its new supporting evidence. Geometry identity, independent solver execution and field semantics remain part of that explanation.

\subsection{Research Questions Answered and Open}
RQ1 is answered descriptively through the genome, decoder, export and analysis identities. RQ2 is answered by concrete contrasts: the highest historical search rank failed later strict assessment; valid plate-study metadata admitted neither GCI estimate; and matched solid computations gave close scalar agreement (Fig.~\ref{fig:verification}). These findings concern different models and evidence questions, and none establishes qualification. RQ3 remains prospective: the corpus supplies no agent-representation ablation, compute-scaling experiment, manufacturing-tolerance study or physical qualification.
