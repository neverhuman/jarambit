\section{Study Design and Retained Evidence}
\label{sec:study-design}
This is a retrospective study of selected retained records, rather than a prospective comparison of optimizers. We ask what those records establish about representation, recorded search preference and later numerical assessment. The three cohorts have different scoring and retention policies; the separately assessed exports supply additional candidate-specific observations, rather than downstream measurements for every search birth. Figure~\ref{fig:evidence-architecture} separates these retained evidence paths. Individual~2000 is a stable publication alias for a retained export: the integer does not state its rank, birth sequence or generation, and its registry entry has no source-birth link.

A \emph{birth} is a recorded candidate-creation event. A \emph{membership} is an appearance in an island slot, including retention or migration of an existing individual. An \emph{archive representative} is a selected record under its archive's objective and descriptor context. An \emph{assessed export} has a specifically bound artifact/assessment bundle. These are different counting units: one genome can occur in several births or memberships, and a certificate identity alone does not establish that its CAD or assessment artifact is retained.

Sources H and P are the historical reconciliation and proposal/screen join accessions; N is the completed generation-800 population accession. E is the automatic manifest plus original field/assessment/verification contracts; C is the controlled-export scorecard accession. Availability is local to these accessions. The native population tables retain all 128 numeric coordinates and material, but their size does not establish complete parentage, per-birth exported CAD, later solid assessment or independent physical replication.

\subsection{Run Identities and Capture Overlap}
The native inventory contains 18 current campaign directories: ten completed generation-800 runs and eight others. An earlier staged snapshot has eleven summary files, of which four are earlier captures of runs later included among the ten completed runs (public Runs~6--9). These are overlapping views of original run identities, not 21 independent runs. Nine aborted crescent attempts are a separate accession; seven retain 216 summary rows in total and two have no retained CSV rows. Summary rows describe generation/island aggregates, not new births. Capture/receipt times are kept separate from unverified historical process start/stop times.

\subsection{Selection and Interpretation}
The selection registry designates Individual~2000 as the illustrated assessed export because its retained bundle connects export geometry, plate fields, mesh-study observations, solid comparison and scorecard. Its recorded human editorial-authority category does not independently identify a named chooser. This illustration role confers neither championship nor qualification. Table~\ref{tab:candidate-evidence} separates it from source-bound stored score leaders and other assessed records. The historical leader illustrates a later-assessment disagreement; neither example is a random sample from the large native archive. Missing observations remain missing; they are not failures, favorable outcomes or a measured qualification fraction.
