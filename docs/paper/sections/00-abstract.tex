Automated design search can retain extensive records while leaving the evidence behind a selected result hard to interpret. We trace one computational result, the titanium hooked utility knife \AllRunsLeader{} shown above, from its executable representation through recorded evolutionary search to its exported solid and assessment. Among \num{\AllRunsStageTwo} comparable Stage-2 assessments from \AllRunsHosts{} hosts, each recomputed by one canonical multi-objective score, it ranks first at \AllRunsLeaderScore{} of 100, and a complete re-assessment gives the same value.\ifdefined\JRSmoothedVariant{} Surface-tension pocket smoothing inside the CAD export turns it into a new solid with clean pocket contours; its full re-assessment gives \PocketSmoothedScore{}.\fi{} The records support \ifdefined\JRSmoothedVariant five\else four\fi{} findings. Evidence coverage depends on the observation stage: \num{\CohortThreeTotalEvaluations} native birth records are not a large assessed population. A search rank is not an assessment outcome: the highest historical birth fails a recorded predicate that a lower-ranked one meets. A score is only as comparable as its scorer: an early evaluator overstated the canonical score of the same genomes by \AllRunsInflationRange{} points. Agreement between two solvers on a shared mesh does not admit extrapolated error estimates.\ifdefined\JRSmoothedVariant{} A geometric post-process is a design change, judged by re-assessment rather than appearance.\fi{} The contribution is an inspectable chain from representation to evidence, with incompatible scores and missing observations kept explicit. All results are computational; the design is not qualified and has not been built.
