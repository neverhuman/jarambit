\subsection{Geometric Smoothing of the Best Evolved Design}
\label{sec:smoothing}
We applied feature-preserving geometric smoothing to the tessellated mesh of the best evolved design by \code{W_ULTRALIGHT_v1}, \code{ef3i17_r05_p14_c06} ($F=75.983539$, 14/18 battery, not qualified), and re-scored the smoothed geometry. Smoothing changes a tessellated mesh, not the STEP/B-rep CAD model.

\textit{Choice of design.} $F$ is recomputed with the canonical scorer (\code{ops/ci/ultralight_weighted_fitness.py}, which agrees with the Rust scorer in \code{auto-evolve}) from each retained Stage-2 scorecard and RS-v2 receipt. Among the 598 retained assessments on 2026-10-22 this design ranks first. An earlier version of this variant smoothed \code{ef2i91_r03_p14_c26}, whose run leaderboard reported 80.253391. Its canonical score, recomputed from its own retained scorecard, is 75.213491. All 218 leaderboard entries of xbabe2's 2026-10-18 run, and 79 of the 108 on xbabe3, report Stage-2 scores above the canonical recomputation, by up to about 5 points, so stored leaderboard values are not used here.

\textit{Method.} The input STL was welded by exact coordinate equality into 45,696 vertices and 91,400 triangles. It is closed, edge-manifold, consistently oriented and connected, with Euler characteristic $-4$. We used the same pre-declared setting as the earlier run: Taubin $\lambda/\mu$ filtering with $\lambda=0.5$ and $\mu=-0.5$ for 20 iterations, keeping only the component of each umbrella vector along the area-weighted unit vertex normal. Every vertex touching one of the 5,706 edges whose two face normals differ by more than $30^\circ$ was pinned (5,668 vertices), and total displacement was capped at 2\,mm. The tool rejects any iteration that reverses a face relative to the input; all 20 iterations completed.

\textit{Re-scoring.} Three of the twenty objectives depend on the exported 3D geometry, and those were re-measured. (1) Mass: the exporter computes 3D mass as enclosed volume times $4.43\,\mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}$. Smoothing changed the enclosed volume by $-14.67\,\mathrm{mm^3}$, giving $91.7483\,\mathrm{g}$ against $91.8133\,\mathrm{g}$. (2) Solid tip: the evaluator builds its P2 TET10 tip submodel (30\,mm cut, four layers) from the exported upper skin. We rebuilt it from the smoothed skin and repeated the \code{scikit-fem} solve and the independent DOLFINx~0.9.0 solve on the same saved mesh (with the pinned solver versions of the original evaluation; an unchanged re-run of the original input reproduces its stored values, $0.3118$ and $0.3135$, exactly). (3) CAD/B-rep: this 5-point objective requires a valid single-solid STEP whose finger-ring bore matches the analytic torus. Smoothing outputs a triangle mesh and moved ring-region vertices by up to 0.229\,mm, so the objective is scored 0. The other seventeen objectives (plate-model responses, sharpness, point profile, ring, holder and aesthetics) are computed from the genome and carry over unchanged. Table~\ref{tab:smoothing-score} gives the result.

\textit{Results.} Smoothing moved 31,180 of 45,696 vertices, 5,038 of them by more than 0.01\,mm. The largest displacement was 0.229\,mm and the RMS displacement 0.0153\,mm. The enclosed volume changed by $-0.071\%$, and the bounding box changed only in $z$, by about 0.012\,mm on each side. The independent solid-tip safety factor at $1.82\,\mathrm{N\,m}$ fell from 0.3118 to 0.3043 at its nodal maximum and rose from 0.3135 to 0.3163 on the nodal average; the two solvers agree within $0.1\%$ on the smoothed mesh. Re-scored, $F$ falls from 75.983539 to 70.977863: $-5$ from the withheld CAD/B-rep objective, $-0.0225$ from the solid tip and $+0.0168$ from mass. Without the withheld objective, smoothing would change $F$ by $-0.005676$. Figure~\ref{fig:smoothing-winner} shows the before and after appearance with the same camera and render settings, and the per-face displacement. The heatmap uses the mean of each face's three corner displacements on a linear scale from 0 to 0.229\,mm; its colours are unlit, so they encode the displacement value.

\textit{Limits.} The smoothed geometry is a triangle mesh. Smoothing does not reconstruct analytic surfaces, establish a continuous Hausdorff bound, test self-intersection, or preserve features beyond the pinned edges. The re-score re-measures only the three objectives that read the exported 3D geometry; the genome-derived objectives were not re-solved on the smoothed shape, whose largest displacement is 0.229\,mm. Neither score is a physical test.
