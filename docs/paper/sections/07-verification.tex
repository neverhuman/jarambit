\section{Numerical Agreement and Unresolved Error}
\label{sec:verification}

\subsection{Model, Load and Boundary Identity}
The plate and local solid records solve linear-elastic static systems $\mathbf K\mathbf u=\mathbf f$ under their respective geometry, material, support and load assumptions. In the plate path, DKT bending supplies lateral response (MPa/N) and chord-torsion response (MPa/(N\,mm)); quadratic T6 membrane elements supply in-plane tip-pry and ring-traction response (MPa/N). Lateral and torsion values are per-triangle maxima over three corner-extrapolated bending stresses; pry and ring values are T6 centroid von Mises stresses. The envelope takes the maximum of lateral and pry responses scaled by $260\,\mathrm{N}$, ring traction scaled by $1500\,\mathrm{N}$, and torsion scaled by $1820\,\mathrm{N\,mm}$ with the producer's global torsion correction. That factor is selected at a peak section and applied globally; it is an approximation rather than a locally recovered three-dimensional torsion field.

The local solid model uses quadratic ($P_2$) displacement fields on linear tetrahedral geometry. It clamps cut-arc vertices and edge-midpoint degrees of freedom to zero. Minimum-norm out-of-plane vertex forces on the $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ tip patch supply $1820\,\mathrm{N\,mm}$ chord torque, with zero resultant force and zero transverse moment. The retained producer does not transfer displacements from a larger model. This cut-arc boundary idealization is shared by both solid implementations.

\subsection{Field Support and Composite Interpretation}
For the $25{,}209$ retained plate triangles, the envelope has $25{,}134$ active cells and $75$ producer-excluded cells within $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ of the boundary tip node. Its saved active peak is $1388.67\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Ring traction has $10{,}334$ active cells and $14{,}875$ producer-zeroed cells in its source-masked half-plane, which extends beyond the seat. The latter are masked; their zeros are not stress measurements. Lateral, tip-pry and raw torsion maps retain all $25{,}209$ cells, including the tip region that is excluded from selected capacity calculations. Source-coordinate rounding makes one ring-boundary cell ambiguous geometrically; its positive stored ring response establishes that the producer retained it.

The response display averages pooled area-weighted percentile ranks over four available channels, with original source masks and genuine finite zeros kept distinct. The derived envelope receives no additional equal weight, and the available-channel mean has no candidate-specific minimum/maximum stretch. Per-cell contributor records and source-area coverage use four eligible channels as the support denominator; the catalog and metadata inventories are not that denominator. Supplement Figure~\ref{S-fig:display-support} shows that support. These dimensionless values locate relatively high responses within the retained fields, rather than safety, fitness or quality. Section~\ref{sec:fitness} and the Supplement distinguish the current display definition from its superseded five-map recipe.

\subsection{Plate Mesh Study: Refused Error Estimates}
The retained three-level study uses maximum triangle areas $4$, $1$ and $0.25\,\mathrm{mm}^2$, corresponding to the nominal mesh-size convention $h=\sqrt{A_{\max}}=2$, $1$ and $0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $3166$, $6803$ and $25{,}209$ elements. This $h$ is defined from the requested maximum area, rather than a measured element-size distribution. Its stored mesh/observable metadata are valid, but the selected relative-GCI procedure~\cite{roache} admits neither error estimate:
\begin{itemize}
\item The yield-order proxy is monotone across the stored sequence but has observed order $p=-2.9003$; the procedure refuses a nonpositive order.
\item The lateral safety-factor sequence has oscillatory differences and is refused on that basis.
\end{itemize}
The stored extrapolated values and fine-grid GCI estimates remain null. This is a failure to establish the assumptions of this retained procedure for these sequences, not a claim that every numerical-error method is impossible. The certification minimum over $0.25/0.125\,\mathrm{mm}^2$ and strict $0.0625\,\mathrm{mm}^2$ scalars are separate assessment stages; they do not supply this three-level study with an additional observation.

\subsection{Shared-Mesh Solid Agreement}
The primary solid path assembles scikit-fem vector $P_2$ tetrahedra~\cite{skfem} and uses PyAMG smoothed aggregation with CG. The comparison path assembles a DOLFINx~0.9.0 vector Lagrange $P_2$ space~\cite{dolfinx} and uses PETSc~3.22.0 CG/GAMG. Both consume the same saved mesh, elastic constants, cut clamp and patch forces. A retained runtime capture lists scikit-fem~12.0.2 and PyAMG~5.3.0; the DOLFINx sidecar records its environment. The capture is written before the orchestration may reuse valid cached solid outputs, and the original solid/verification records do not bind its digest. It therefore documents a captured environment, without proving the exact environment of a cached historical solve. The Supplement gives the full captured versions and image identity separately from the assembly and solver records.

Both recover von Mises stress at element vertices and form the incident-cell maximum or unweighted mean. The comparison region is $4\le d_{\mathrm{tip}}<15\,\mathrm{mm}$ with distance from the saved cut vertices $>3\,\mathrm{mm}$. The saved primary safety factors have three decimal places ($0.669$, $0.726$), whereas the comparison has four ($0.6693$, $0.7263$), under maximum and mean recovery respectively. With $\delta=|\mathrm{SF}_{\mathrm{DOLFINx}}/\mathrm{SF}_{\mathrm{scikit\mbox{-}fem}}-1|$, those stored rounded scalars give differences of $\SolverAgreementMaxPct{}\%$ and $0.0413\%$. These are comparisons of the reported safety-factor tokens, not bounds on unrounded agreement or physical accuracy. The primary record reports $152$ iterations and relative residual $8.80\times10^{-11}$; the comparison reports $158$ iterations.

This comparison cross-checks two assembly/solver implementations through selected recovered scalar observables under shared inputs. Common mesh preparation, material, boundary and load assumptions can carry common-mode errors. The comparison does not verify every algebraic operation, bound discretization or modeling error, establish mesh independence, or validate the clamped submodel against physical measurements. The retained profile bins use different plate-cell and recovered-solid-vertex observables and therefore require their own interpretation.

\subsection{Open Eigenproblem and Assurance Definitions}
The support study's eigenvalue is not admitted here as a current engineering buckling result. Its producer invokes a symmetric eigensolver on $\mathbf K_s^{-1}\mathbf G$. Symmetric component matrices alone do not establish Euclidean self-adjointness of that product, and no confirming operator-symmetry check or eigenpair residual is retained. The eigensolver's operator contract~\cite{eigsh} identifies the unresolved question; it does not supply a historical validation result. The Supplement preserves the original scalar and status as historical provenance. Any qualification inference that depends on this eigenvalue remains unestablished; this reporting revision neither replaces the value nor rescores the historical observations.

The frozen assurance catalog defines $178$ challenges, or $356$ paired positive/negative control slots across 20 families. Those are definitions, not proof that all slots executed. Individual execution receipts and outcomes must establish which controls ran; unavailable receipts remain unavailable. Neither catalog size nor repeated software-control labels supplies additional candidate-specific mechanical evidence.
