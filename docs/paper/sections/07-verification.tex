\section{Numerical Agreement and Unresolved Error}
\label{sec:verification}

\subsection{Model, Load and Boundary Identity}
The plate and local solid records solve linear-elastic static systems $\mathbf K\mathbf u=\mathbf f$ under their respective geometry, material, support and load assumptions. In the plate path, DKT bending supplies lateral response (MPa/N) and chord-torsion response (MPa/(N\,mm)); quadratic T6 membrane elements supply in-plane tip-pry and ring-traction response (MPa/N). Lateral and torsion values are per-triangle maxima over three corner-extrapolated bending stresses; pry and ring values are T6 centroid von Mises stresses. The envelope takes the maximum of lateral and pry responses scaled by $260\,\mathrm{N}$, ring traction scaled by $1500\,\mathrm{N}$, and torsion scaled by $1820\,\mathrm{N\,mm}$ with the producer's global torsion correction.

The local solid model uses quadratic ($P_2$) displacement fields on linear tetrahedral geometry. It clamps cut-arc vertices and edge-midpoint degrees of freedom to zero. Minimum-norm out-of-plane vertex forces on the $2\,\mathrm{mm}$ tip patch supply $1820\,\mathrm{N\,mm}$ chord torque, with zero resultant force and zero transverse moment. The retained producer does not transfer displacements from a larger model. This cut-arc boundary idealization is shared by both solid implementations.

\subsection{Field Support and Composite Interpretation}
For the $25{,}209$ retained plate triangles, the envelope has $25{,}134$ active cells and $75$ producer-excluded cells within $4\,\mathrm{mm}$ of the boundary tip node. Its saved active peak is $1388.67\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Ring traction has $10{,}334$ active cells and $14{,}875$ producer-zeroed seated cells. The latter are masked; their zeros are not stress measurements. Lateral, tip-pry and raw torsion maps retain all $25{,}209$ cells, including the tip region that is excluded from selected capacity calculations. Source-coordinate rounding makes one ring-boundary cell ambiguous geometrically; its positive stored ring response establishes that the producer retained it.

The response display averages pooled area-weighted percentile ranks over four available channels, with original source masks and genuine finite zeros kept distinct. The derived envelope receives no additional equal weight, and the available-channel mean has no candidate-specific minimum/maximum stretch. Per-cell contributor records and source-area coverage use four eligible channels as the support denominator; the catalog and metadata inventories are not that denominator. Supplement Figure~\ref{S-fig:display-support} shows that support. These dimensionless values locate relatively high responses within the retained fields, rather than safety, fitness or quality. Section~\ref{sec:fitness} and the Supplement distinguish the current display definition from its superseded five-map recipe.

\subsection{Plate Mesh Study: Refused Error Estimates}
The retained three-level study uses maximum triangle areas $4$, $1$ and $0.25\,\mathrm{mm}^2$, corresponding to $h=\sqrt{A_{\max}}=2$, $1$ and $0.5\,\mathrm{mm}$ and $3166$, $6803$ and $25{,}209$ elements. Its stored mesh/observable metadata are valid, but the selected relative-GCI procedure~\cite{roache} admits neither error estimate:
\begin{itemize}
\item The yield-order proxy is monotone across the stored sequence but has observed order $p=-2.9003$; the procedure refuses a nonpositive order.
\item The lateral safety-factor sequence has oscillatory differences and is refused on that basis.
\end{itemize}
The stored extrapolated values and fine-grid GCI estimates remain null. This is a failure to establish the assumptions of this retained procedure for these sequences, not a claim that every numerical-error method is impossible. The certification minimum over $0.25/0.125\,\mathrm{mm}^2$ and strict $0.0625\,\mathrm{mm}^2$ scalars are separate assessment stages; they do not supply this three-level study with an additional observation.

\subsection{Shared-Mesh Solid Agreement}
The primary solid path assembles scikit-fem vector $P_2$ tetrahedra~\cite{skfem} and uses PyAMG smoothed aggregation with CG. The comparison path assembles a DOLFINx~0.9.0 vector Lagrange $P_2$ space~\cite{dolfinx} and uses PETSc~3.22.0 CG/GAMG. Both consume the same saved mesh, elastic constants, cut clamp and patch forces. The scikit-fem/PyAMG executed package versions are not retained; the DOLFINx sidecar records its environment. The implementation table in the Supplement distinguishes these records from Rust orchestration.

Both recover von Mises stress at element vertices and form the incident-cell maximum or unweighted mean. The comparison region is $4\le d_{\mathrm{tip}}<15\,\mathrm{mm}$ with distance from the saved cut vertices $>3\,\mathrm{mm}$. The saved safety factors are $0.669$ versus $0.6693$ under maximum recovery and $0.726$ versus $0.7263$ under mean recovery. With $\delta=|\mathrm{SF}_{\mathrm{DOLFINx}}/\mathrm{SF}_{\mathrm{scikit\mbox{-}fem}}-1|$, those rounded scalars give differences of $0.0448\%$ and $0.0413\%$. The primary record reports $152$ iterations and relative residual $8.80\times10^{-11}$; the comparison reports $158$ iterations.

This agreement supports consistency of the selected recovered scalar observables under shared inputs. It does not verify every algebraic operation, bound discretization or modeling error, establish mesh independence, or validate the clamped submodel against physical measurements. The retained profile bins use different plate-cell and recovered-solid-vertex observables and therefore require their own interpretation.

\subsection{Open Eigenproblem and Assurance Definitions}
The support study's stored eigenvalue ($7.035$) is provisional. Its producer invokes a symmetric eigensolver on $\mathbf K_s^{-1}\mathbf G$. Symmetric component matrices alone do not establish Euclidean self-adjointness of that product, and no confirming operator-symmetry check or eigenpair residual is retained. The eigensolver's operator contract~\cite{eigsh} identifies the unresolved question; it does not supply a historical validation result.

The frozen assurance catalog defines $178$ challenges, or $356$ paired positive/negative control slots across 20 families. Those are definitions, not proof that all slots executed. Individual execution receipts and outcomes must establish which controls ran; unavailable receipts remain unavailable. Neither catalog size nor repeated software-control labels supplies additional candidate-specific mechanical evidence.
