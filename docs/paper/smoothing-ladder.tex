\medskip
\textbf{What this shows.} The exported triangle mesh of the design is smoothed at ten strengths, from none (L0) to the strongest setting the smoother completes on its knife edges without reversing a face (L9). Each level is re-scored with the canonical \code{W_ULTRALIGHT_v1} and rendered from one fixed oblique camera under one low raking light, with the thickness direction exaggerated and faces coloured by height, so that grinds, serrations, channels and faceting stand out. Every camera, light, colour range and exaggeration is shared by all levels; image differences are mesh differences.

\textbf{Smoothing.} Taubin $\lambda/\mu$ filtering ($\lambda=0.5$, $\mu=-0.5$), keeping only the component of each umbrella vector along the area-weighted vertex normal, total displacement capped at 2\,mm. Vertices on edges whose two face normals differ by more than the pin angle do not move. L1--L5 pin at $30^\circ$ (L4, 20 iterations, is the R1 setting of paper variant B); L6--L9 pin at $60^\circ$, the weakest pin angle that completes on this mesh.

\textbf{Re-scoring.} Three of the twenty objectives read the exported 3D geometry and are re-measured at every level: 3D mass (enclosed volume times $4.43\,\mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}$), the solid tip (scikit-fem P2 submodel on the smoothed skin, cross-checked by DOLFINx~0.9.0 on the same mesh) and the CAD/B-rep objective. The B-rep objective is withheld (scored 0) on every smoothed level, because a smoothed triangle mesh has no validated STEP solid; that costs 5 points at once, so the table also gives $F$ with the B-rep objective kept as exported, which isolates the effect of smoothing. The other seventeen objectives are computed from the genome and carry over unchanged: in particular, edge sharpness and point profile are \emph{not} re-measured on the smoothed mesh. Neither score is a physical test.
