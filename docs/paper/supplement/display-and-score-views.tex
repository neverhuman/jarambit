\subsection{Complete Inventory and Spatial Availability}
The complete row-level table is retained as
\nolinkurl{docs/paper/generated/test-field-roster.v1.tsv}, with its source-bound
JSON contract in \nolinkurl{docs/paper/data/test-field-roster.v1.json}.
Every row gives its original key, scientific quantity, observation stage,
availability and exclusion or missing-field reason. The JSON additionally
binds source observations, hashes, domains, masks and dependencies.
Table~\ref{tab:display-roster-families} reconciles its universe. Inventory
membership does not imply an independent test, performed solve or spatial
response. Scalar-only and incompatible-domain rows remain in the table and
contribute missing values at every display cell; they are never broadcast.
The requested multihue palette has no monotone-lightness or universal
color-vision accessibility claim. Grayscale and simulated color-vision
diagnostics can merge different index colors. Numeric per-cell values,
contributor records and the separate physical-unit views remain necessary
for quantitative interpretation; hue alone is insufficient.

\subsection{Source-Coordinate Crops and Whole-Field Denominators}
Main Figure~\ref{M-fig:plate-fields} changes only the viewing window.
For each eligible original field, a range cutoff is
$t_\alpha=v_{\min}+\alpha(v_{\max}-v_{\min})$ with fixed candidates
$\alpha=0.50,0.75,0.90$; it is not a count or area quantile.
The baseline $t_{50}$ always comes from the whole original eligible field,
including when a more concentrated cutoff proposes the window.
For original triangle areas $A_i$ and their exact rectangularly clipped
areas $A_i^{\mathrm{crop}}$, the area and excess fractions are
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\sum_i A_i^{\mathrm{crop}}}{\sum_i A_i},
 \qquad
 \frac{\sum_i A_i^{\mathrm{crop}}(v_i-t_{50})_+}
      {\sum_i A_i(v_i-t_{50})_+}.
\end{equation}
Both sums use the whole source-eligible set after its original masks;
missing values are not zeros, and genuine finite zeros remain valid.
Positive excess is display saliency, not physical energy.
The saliency-selection policy requires at least $95\%$ of that whole-field excess,
a bounding-box fraction at most $0.40$ and scale gain at least $1.75$,
with padding fraction $0.08$. These are display constants, not physical
or qualification gates; $95\%$ is a policy threshold, not a reported
achieved coverage. All threshold ties, maxima and disconnected support
participate. Missing, constant or insufficiently concentrated fields
retain full context; undefined fractions remain missing.
No crop-local color normalization is applied.
For the lateral and torsional B/D panels, a separate display camera expands
the selected ROI to at least $30\%$ of the whole object's horizontal extent,
with physical-aspect padding and clamping to the original source bounds.
This wider camera shows curved distal blade/tip context. Its area and excess
fractions are recomputed from the exact clipped original triangles; the
smaller saliency-selection ROI retains its own threshold and statistics.
The PiP red rectangle is the actual displayed camera, including this context,
not the smaller selected ROI. The $0.40$ compactness requirement above applies
to the saliency selection, not to the expanded context camera.
The source-bound \nolinkurl{docs/paper/generated/plate-smart-crops.v1.json}
records the actual decision, thresholds, source hashes, clipped areas,
whole-field integrals and affine crop/locator coordinates for each panel.

\subsection{Bounded Run~5 Display Comparison}
For a within-run point view, Figure~\ref{fig:run5-gap} maps every retained Run~5 birth using $x_+=g+1$ and $z=(b-f)/s+\varepsilon$, displayed on base-10 logarithmic axes. Here $b=1.1440$ is the fixed final best retained score of Run~5, $s=f_{\max}-f_{\min}$ is its full original numeric range and $\varepsilon=10^{-6}$ is a display floor. The inverse is $f=b-s(z-\varepsilon)$; generation is recovered by $g=x_+-1$. Vertical ticks recover original stored fitness, and zero and negative scores remain admitted. The reference is retrospective, not the current running best or an unknown optimum; the floor permits zero original gap and is not a measured residual. Shifted-gap ratios and plotted slopes have no physical performance interpretation.

Figures~\ref{fig:run5-display-comparison}, \ref{fig:run5-positive-affine}
and~\ref{fig:run5-gap-summary} reuse the same retained cumulative-best
and full-population median summaries. These summary panels draw no raw
birth points. Figure~\ref{fig:run5-gap} separately projects every
Run~5 birth and derives its cumulative-best overlay from those raw rows,
without a median overlay. The full numeric point universe and its $1390$
nonnumeric proposals remain in main Figure~\ref{fig:population-latest};
none is reassigned a display-floor score.
Each comparison uses the complete compatible original Run~5 range,
rather than deriving normalization from the summary trace or a crop.
Exact parameters, source membership and unchanged leader ties accompany
the generated story receipts.

The separate all-point positive-coordinate set keeps twelve source scopes
apart: ten native runs, historical births and numeric screening observations,
with positive-affine and positive-gap variants for each. The proof index
\nolinkurl{docs/paper/generated/population/positive/index.v1.json} binds
their source membership, point accounting, display parameters and outputs.
This is a set of separate display domains, not a common score calibration.
The raw-point variants use a navy--teal--yellow--orange--red key for original
stored scores, with a monotone contrast parameter derived from the declared
logarithmic coordinate and reversed when needed so that warmer means better
within the named run/stage/profile. Original-unit colorbar ticks and the exact
contrast definition accompany each view. Lower screening deficit is better;
no color scale pools its values with native fitness. Later original-source
marks can overpaint earlier colors, including overlapping fixed point footprints;
the key encodes the last painted original score, not density or a pixel average.
The cumulative-best curve keeps the same coordinates with a dark stroke and
light halo. These RGB changes do not rescore any observation.
Its full admitted universe remains $1{,}958{,}803$ numeric observations;
$1390$ missing screens remain nonnumeric. Screening retains its one-based
birth-file ordinal rather than an invented generation or shared campaign
trajectory. These views add no observations or physical convergence evidence.
