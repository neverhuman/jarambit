\subsection{Actual Field-Ranking Display Recipe}

Equal serialized values are visited in deterministic source-cell order, not pooled into a single tied-value rank. It averages those ranks over active maps per cell, then applies a candidate-specific affine stretch from the resulting minimum/maximum to $[0,1]$.
