\section{Complete Historical 23-Subject, 18-Evaluator CAD Battery}
\label{sup:battery-matrix}

\subsection{A Matrix of Different Evidence Kinds}
The historical battery contains 23 retained subjects and 18 evaluator columns. Its 414 cells include software capability, geometry binding, numerical findings, shared reports, and record completeness. The manifest fixes membership. A later automatic assessment is a separate corpus and does not silently create a twenty-fourth subject. All cells in this historical corpus report \code{fresh\_this\_lane: false}.

The stored statuses reconcile to \BatteryPass\ passes (221), \BatteryFail\ failures (81), and \BatteryBlocked\ blocked records (112). These are labels from heterogeneous producers. They are preserved here as historical data alongside the exact explanation of what each label meant. In particular, the four shared build columns account for 92 stored passes ($4 \times 23$), and the local-solid column's 20 favorable statuses concern record completeness. Neither fact supports an aggregate claim of 221 independently successful physical engineering tests.

Figure~\ref{fig:battery-matrix} retains every subject/evaluator pairing in manifest order. Each subsection below refers to the column with its exact evaluator ID and \code{docs/tests/README.md} anchor. Letters accompany the colors so missing evidence remains distinguishable from an unfavorable observation. Source-cell selectors, original kinds, and evidence-path strings remain in the linked projection (\code{docs/paper/generated/battery-matrix.json}); the aggregate table remains in the artifact package. The manifest's source tag is not uniform across its members: 21 subject records cite \code{6d18ef8c}, while the two \code{w283} records cite \code{4e54019d}. These are retained provenance fields, not a newly verified homogeneous solver regime.

\subsection{Exhaustive Column-by-Column Historical Battery Reference (18 Evaluators)}

\test{build.analytic: Analytic Software Controls (\code{battery-build.analytic})}
This column references shared analytic/reference software controls (\src{crates/physics/tests/invariance.rs}{\code{invariance.rs}}). Such controls can expose errors in units, invariance, assembly, or solver behavior on reference problems. A per-subject copy of a shared control status does not establish another execution. The historical 23 passes should therefore be understood as repeated references to capability evidence. They do not establish candidate-specific geometry validity or physical behavior.

\test{build.gci\_contracts: Convergence Arithmetic (\code{battery-build.gci\_contracts})}
This column concerns software rules for interpreting mesh-study data (\src{fem_solid/tests/test_gci.py}{\code{test\_gci.py}}). Positive and negative controls demonstrate that a formula or refusal condition is implemented as intended. It does not mean that a particular CAD has a suitable three-level study, matching observables, or an admitted asymptotic error estimate. Candidate-specific convergence belongs to \code{plate.gci}.

\test{build.claims: Reporting and Admission (\code{battery-build.claims})}
This column references controls over how evidence is accepted, refused, and rendered into claims (\src{ops/ci/check_claims.py}{\code{check\_claims.py}}). Its object is the reporting system. A favorable software outcome establishes behavior of that control under its fixture; it does not establish the mechanics of any CAD or authorize an unscoped public safety statement.

\test{build.dolfinx: Separate-Solver Capability (\code{battery-build.dolfinx})}
The retained column references shared solver-comparison evidence (\code{evidence/cad\_battery/dolfinx\_cross\_check.json}). It indicates the scope of that shared capability record, not a fresh dual-solver computation for every subject. Candidate-specific comparison requires the two actual execution identities, matched problem specification, and observable correspondence (\code{G16-16}).

\test{geom.unit: Export Units (\code{battery-geom.unit})}
The unit column (\src{ops/ci/verify_genome_export.py}{\code{verify\_genome\_export.py}}) asks whether the exported representation's unit interpretation is established by the relevant evidence. All 23 historical cells are blocked. This does not prove that every export has incorrect units; it means the required binding was not established in those historical records.

\test{geom.one\_solid: Topology Binding (\code{battery-geom.one\_solid})}
This column (\src{ops/ci/verify_genome_export.py}{\code{verify\_genome\_export.py}}) records whether single-solid topology was established for the subject. Its 23 blocked cells preserve a missing-evidence condition rather than 23 unfavorable topology measurements. A mesh closure check and a B-rep solid check do not establish the same fact.

\test{geom.mass: Mass Observation (\code{battery-geom.mass})}
The mass column (\src{crates/crescent/src/battery.rs}{\code{battery.rs}}) has 19 retained genome observations and four blocked reference entries. For those genome entries, the historical value is a plate/certification mass observation, not a demonstrated STEP-versus-raster consistency comparison.

\test{geom.pin: Geometry Identity (\code{battery-geom.pin})}
This column (\src{crates/crescent/src/cad_ingest.rs}{\code{cad\_ingest.rs}}) concerns binding the observation to the intended CAD via \code{geometry\_sha256}. All 23 historical entries are blocked because filenames alone cannot establish cryptographic identity.

\test{plate.loads: Plate Response Finding (\code{battery-plate.loads})}
The load column (\src{crates/crescent/src/fem.rs}{\code{fem.rs}}) reports the stored plate-response comparison: 19 unfavorable records and 4 blocked reference entries. Unfavorable findings are results of the model and comparison policy, not solver crashes; blocked entries are missing records, not measured failures.

\test{plate.seq: Regional Response Comparison (\code{battery-plate.seq})}
The sequence column (\src{crates/crescent/src/fem.rs}{\code{fem.rs}}) records 10 stored favorable and 9 unfavorable findings among genome entries (plus 4 blocked reference entries), inheriting the linear-elastic proxy limitations of \code{G16-14}.

\test{plate.l7: Fatigue Screen (\code{battery-plate.l7})}
The fatigue column (\src{crates/crescent/src/fem.rs}{\code{fem.rs}}) records 19 unfavorable model assessments and 4 blocked entries. It is a disclosed analytical screen, not a physical lifetime test.

\test{plate.gci: Candidate Mesh Study (\code{battery-plate.gci})}
This column (\src{fem_solid/gci.py}{\code{gci.py}}) reports candidate-specific convergence evidence: 4 favorable, 15 unfavorable, and 4 blocked records, distinct from the software arithmetic controls in \code{build.gci\_contracts}.

\test{side.a1: Shared Side-Report Reference (\code{battery-side.a1})}
References a shared retained side report (\src{crates/crescent/examples/g3_side_report.rs}{\code{g3\_side\_report.rs}}): 19 favorable references and 4 blocked entries, representing report availability under the historical producer's convention rather than a distinct executed experiment.

\test{side.a2: Shared Side-Report Reference (\code{battery-side.a2})}
Similarly references the common side report (19 favorable, 4 blocked); no distinct physical mechanism or separate execution is established solely by this row.

\test{side.a3: Shared Side-Report Reference (\code{battery-side.a3})}
Has the same evidential scope as \code{side.a1} and \code{side.a2} (19 favorable, 4 blocked). Treating A1, A2, and A3 as three independent successful experiments would manufacture corroboration from table structure.

\test{side.a4: Support and Stability Report (\code{battery-side.a4})}
Records the alternate-support/stability report (\src{ops/ci/side_a4.py}{\code{side\_a4.py}}): 19 stored passes and 4 blocked entries. Report completeness, a favorable numerical finding, and physical support calibration remain distinct, and the \code{eigsh} $\mathbf{K}_s^{-1}\mathbf{G}$ symmetry/residual audit remains open.

\test{solid.tip: Solid-Result Completeness (\code{battery-solid.tip})}
Twenty stored passes and three blocked records (\src{fem_solid/tip_solid.py}{\code{tip\_solid.py}}). The 20 stored passes mean that local-solid result records were complete under the column's rules, not 20 favorable strength comparisons.

\test{gate.rs\_v2: Combined Grouped Finding (\code{battery-gate.rs\_v2})}
Records the combined historical RS-v2 finding (\src{crates/cli/src/bench.rs}{\code{bench.rs}}): 19 failures and 4 blocked reference entries, with zero passes.
