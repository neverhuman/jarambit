## Interpretation and editorial checks

- Table I compares representation and evaluation scopes; it is not a common benchmark or a quantitative ranking. Every external row is supported by the adjacent cited prose.
- Learned CAD generation, execution-based program search and mechanical assessment answer different questions. The revision does not treat a CAD generator as a validated engineering agent.
- The retrospective result remains the search-versus-assessment contrast and the distinct scopes of numerical agreement and convergence evidence. No new optimizer, geometry, experiment or performance comparison is introduced.
- Introductory terminology is now *intelligent computational design*. Repeated author-facing publication instructions were removed from the introduction/discussion, while the local evidence limitations remain beside the affected results.
- Release still requires a broader claim/reference audit, independent reconstruction/review and venue selection. This register does not close RT-04 visual coverage, RT-10 public PDF delivery or the A4 eigensolver finding.
