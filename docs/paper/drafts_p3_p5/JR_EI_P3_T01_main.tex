\noindent\textbf{Abstract---}Engineered Intelligence makes a physical design process executable and its evolution inspectable. We present Jarambit, a computational design system connecting a 128-coordinate continuous genome and categorical alloy state, deterministic CAD decoding, island-based evolutionary search, and multi-model finite-element verification. Rather than reducing computational design to a single terminal shape, the retained record exposes both candidate geometries and the population dynamics that generated them across three distinct experimental cohorts. In Cohort~3 (10-Run 800-Gen Population, \CohortThreeTotalEvaluations\ Evaluations), ten retained four-island quality-diversity runs (\text{Run~2} through \text{Run~11}) each evaluate \CohortThreeRowsPerRun\ births across \CohortThreeGenerations\ generations ($0$--$800$), yielding \CohortThreeTotalEvaluations\ complete 129-field genome-and-material records (\CohortThreeLegalEvaluations\ satisfying search-level geometric legality, sample mean $75{,}423.6 \pm 2{,}936.55$ per run). In Cohort~1 (Historical 17-Gen, \text{Run~1}), \CohortOneBirths\ retained births across generations $0$--$16$ (\CohortOneFemComplete\ with completed plate finite-element solves and \CohortOneFemNotEvaluated\ not evaluated by finite elements) preserve complete parent links, 2,176 four-island slot memberships, 192 elite reappearances, and 12 inter-island migrations at generations 4, 8, and 12. Because search fitness combines legality penalties with archive-contextual novelty, the highest birth score in \text{Run~1} (\CohortOneBestFit, Individual~\CohortOneBestId\ at generation~\CohortOneBestGen) fails subsequent strict multi-model verification, demonstrating why search preference and engineering qualification must remain distinct. Cohort~2 (Intermediate Screening) retains \CohortTwoBirthEvents\ multi-stage birth proposals (\CohortTwoScreenRows\ joined to Stage-1 screening observations and \CohortTwoUnscreenedBirths\ preserved in an explicit unscreened observation band). For the illustrated assessed geometry (Individual~2000; assessed in the 23-subject automatic CAD battery and not qualified, with zero qualified designs in the assessed set and the \CohortThreeTotalEvaluations\ native search births not individually Stage-2 CAD-assessed), source-bound plate response fields ($25{,}209$ triangles across $13{,}503$ nodes covering five structural demand maps plus safety-factor and thickness fields) and matched-mesh quadratic solid submodels (solver agreement within $0.05\%$, with plate grid-convergence estimates refused under non-monotonic/negative-order criteria) link spatial mechanics directly to decoded CAD topology without claiming physical qualification.
