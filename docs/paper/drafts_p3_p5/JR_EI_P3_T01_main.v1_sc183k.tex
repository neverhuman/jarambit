\noindent\textbf{Abstract---}Engineered Intelligence makes a physical design process executable and its evolution inspectable. We present Jarambit, a Rust-centered system connecting versioned numerical genomes, deterministic CAD decoding, automated evolutionary search, and multi-stage numerical assessment. Rather than reducing computational design to a single selected image, its retained histories expose both candidate geometries and the population process that produced them. A reconstructed historical experiment (\code{w282\_ultrarunner}) contains \BirthCount\ retained births across 17 generations (\FemComplete\ with completed plate finite-element observations and \FemNotEvaluated\ recorded as not evaluated), preserving parent relationships, elite retention, four-island membership observations, and inter-island migration. Because the historical search objective combines legality-dependent penalty branches with archive-contextual novelty, the highest stored birth fitness (\HistoricalBestFit, Individual~\HistoricalBestId\ at generation~\HistoricalBestGeneration) does not pass later strict assessment, demonstrating why search preference and mechanical verification must remain distinct. We complement this reconstructable genealogy with two separately bounded repository cohorts: \NewerBirthEventsCount\ newer birth events across \NewerBirthFilesCount\ files (\NewerScreenRowsCount\ joined via one-based candidate identity to screening records and \NewerUnscreenedBirthsCount\ without a screen row) and \NativeSummaryRowsCount\ headerless generation-by-island summary rows across \NativeSummaryFilesCount\ staged native quality-diversity histories (reaching generation~\NativeLongestRetainedGen\ in the initial staged subset, alongside \CompletedGenEightHundredRunsCount\ generation-800 host runs undergoing lossless full-population accession). For the separately retained assessed export \code{cert\_pipeline\_r01\_p14\_c02}, source-bound spatial plate fields and matched-mesh solid-solver safety factors (agreeing within $0.05\%$ while plate grid-convergence index estimates remain unadmitted) connect numerical observations to exact exported geometry without claiming physical qualification. The contribution is a reproducible account of physical design as an evolving computation: executable representations generate alternatives, recorded inheritance explains their relationships, and traceable observations keep every claim open to independent inspection.
