\section{Introduction: Engineered Intelligence as an Executable Design Process}
\label{sec:introduction}
\IEEEPARstart{E}{ngineering} design is traditionally documented through its terminal artifact: a finished CAD assembly, a selected stress contour, and a summary table asserting that requirements were met. When evolutionary computation and automated numerical modeling enter the loop, that artifact-only view conceals the primary scientific object. The primary object is no longer a single hand-tuned shape, but an executable design process that generates, evaluates, retains, and relates thousands of candidate geometries under explicit numerical preferences.

We define \emph{Engineered Intelligence} operationally: a versioned numerical representation, an executable geometric decoder, a recorded evolutionary process, declared numerical preferences, and an inspectable history of decisions and observations. Presenting this category operationally avoids two common pitfalls. First, the category name is not a claim of a new optimization theorem, autonomous agent invention, or universal superiority over human engineers. Second, it prevents assessment administration and internal test taxonomies from crowding out the evolutionary mechanism that actually produces candidate designs.

Jarambit provides a concrete computational testbed for Engineered Intelligence. Its crescent representation encodes candidate geometry in 128 continuous floating-point genes paired with a categorical material state. Decoding a genome produces an identifiable B-rep solid and analysis discretization, which are evaluated through plate and local quadratic-solid finite-element models, geometric and reference-shape diagnostics, and software-completeness controls \cite{repo}. Across its retained history, the repository preserves multiple distinct evolutionary and reporting cohorts alongside source-bound spatial response fields.

\subsection{Reading Population Legends Before Results}
\label{subsec:population-legend-conventions}
Every population and trajectory figure in this study enforces four visual and semantic conventions defined before the first data plate appears:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Score Regime and Direction:} Each plotted series binds to a single declared score profile and stage (\code{score\_profiles.v1.json}). Historical native search fitness $f$ (higher is better in raw logs, with conditional legality/feasibility branches and contemporaneous archive novelty) is never spliced onto Stage-1 screening or Stage-2 weighted reporting scores $F \in [0,100]$. Where a descending display loss ($L = -f$ or $L = 100 - F$) is shown, the axis explicitly declares the reversible transform alongside native values.
  \item \textbf{Entity Separation:} A birth observation (one evaluation of a newly generated individual at its birth generation), a population membership occurrence (an individual occupying one of the $4 \times 32$ island slots after contextual rescoring and before migration), and a generation-end summary row (\code{GenEnd} or \code{history\_live.csv} aggregate) are plotted as distinct layers. Monotone cumulative-best envelopes summarize historical maxima; they do not imply that every birth or surviving island member improved.
  \item \textbf{Evaluation Status vs.\ Missingness:} Completed FEM evaluations (\code{complete}), unevaluated geometries (\code{not\_evaluated} / historical \code{notevaluated}), unscreened newer births, and missing refreshed elite scores are rendered in separate visual bands. No missing observation is imputed as numerical zero or reinterpreted as a solver failure.
  \item \textbf{Public Identity vs.\ Selection Role:} Public integer identifiers (\code{public\_ids.v1.json}) distinguish evolutionary births from controlled probes and retained exports. In particular, the cover's assessed export (\code{cert\_pipeline\_r01\_p14\_c02}) is an illustrated assessed individual with \code{champion=null}, not the termination of the historical \code{w282} birth cloud.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Contributions and Evidence Boundary}
\label{subsec:contributions}
This work makes four contributions within an explicit retrospective evidence boundary:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Executable Process Architecture:} We formulate and document an end-to-end Engineered Intelligence pipeline linking a 128-gene + material representation, CAD decoding, island-based evolutionary search, and multi-fidelity numerical evaluation with cryptographic content addressing (\code{jarambit.science.canonical.v1}).
  \item \textbf{Multi-Cohort Population Reconstruction:} We reconstruct and reconcile the complete 1,984-birth historical experiment (genealogy, 2,176 slot memberships, and migrations), 3,649 newer birth records with validated one-based screen joins, and 17,672 native generation-by-island summary rows, establishing exact provenance boundaries between birth events, surviving archive cells, and aggregate summaries.
  \item \textbf{Source-Bound Numerical Methods and 44-Entry Crosswalk:} We organize the mechanical, geometric, convergence, and software-control diagnostics into eleven self-contained methodological families tied to exact retained spatial fields and scalar pairs for \code{cert\_pipeline\_r01\_p14\_c02}, while preserving all 414 historical battery outcomes and all 44 test-atlas anchors in a structured supplement crosswalk.
  \item \textbf{Compact Reproducibility and Recovery Specification:} We specify versioned schemas for lossless IEEE-754 genome transport, append-only zstd event segments, immutable snapshot manifests, and engine-specific continuation versus warm-start/fork semantics across multi-worker compute nodes.
\end{enumerate}

\emph{Scope and Non-Claims:} All empirical results in this manuscript are computational and retrospective, anchored to source baseline \code{009f5ac520fa3afe8ed8025230eef604dbb7f922} and its audited archive handoffs. No candidate in the retained corpus is physically qualified (\code{champion=null}), no physical prototyping or mechanical testing was conducted for this study, and no claim is made of autonomous AI representation invention, completed four-worker production live-resume rollout, or universal superiority over human design. Planned interactive viewer, v3 white-studio composite rendering, and production checkpoint-recovery modules are identified as engineering specifications until their final validation receipts are sealed in Phase~P8.
