\section{Search}\label{sec:search}
\subsection{Archive, start and variation}
Each island is a MAP-Elites archive~\cite{mouret2015,vassiliades2018} of 1,024 niches in an eight-dimensional behaviour
space (\src{crates/g3/src/g3_qd.rs}{\code{CvtMapElitesArchive}}). The niche centres are the first 1,024 points of a
low-discrepancy Halton sequence (rather than Lloyd-relaxed CVT centroids), so every island shares the same partition. The descriptors are hook drop, inner turn angle, silhouette
complexity, grip section height-to-width ratio, finger-groove articulation, porosity, surface-texture energy and mass.
The last three are re-measured on the voxel model at Tier~1 and above; the other five come from the genes and the 2D
profile.

An island starts from 64 Joe--Kuo Sobol points in $[0,1]^{179}$~\cite{joe2008}, scrambled with a random linear matrix
and a digital shift~\cite{matousek1998}, and from nothing else. Afterwards each evaluation breeds one child from two
archived parents. With probability 0.4 both parents are drawn from the 16 best-scoring elites; otherwise each parent
wins a tournament of three on curiosity, a niche score that rises when the niche's offspring enter the archive and
decays when they do not.
\code{reproduce_iso_line_dd} then combines three steps:
\begin{itemize}
\item with probability 0.15, a crossover that swaps one whole gene module from the second parent;
\item self-adaptive step sizes, one per gene module, carried in five genes of the genome (0.01--0.20; four mapped to active module step sizes and the fifth reserved) and mutated
log-normally;
\item an iso-line step: Gaussian noise at the module's step size plus $0.25\,\mathcal{N}(0,1)$ times the difference to
the second parent.
\end{itemize}
A child takes its nearest niche if the niche is empty, if the child was evaluated at a higher tier than the incumbent,
or if it scores higher at the same tier (\code{CvtMapElitesArchive::insert}).

\subsection{Three-tier evaluation}
To spend finite-element time on promising designs, every child passes through a three-tier cascade.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Tier~0, analytic.} Closed-form geometry and beam screens on the decoded 2D profiles, including a
lateral-torsional buckling estimate, take a few milliseconds. A design scoring at least \gGateOne{} goes on to Tier~1.
\item \textbf{Tier~1, voxel model and plate.} A voxel SDF of the whole knife and its lattice at $h = \gH$~mm
(\code{build_g3_sdf}) gives mass, centre of mass, spin inertia and the grip section. One sandwich-plate finite-element
mesh of the blade (CST membrane and DKT bending~\cite{batoz1980}, meshed on the outer silhouette \code{mesh_domain(\&b_outer, \&[], 0.9)} without subtracting internal fuller cutouts) gives the lateral capacity under a unit load, the blade twist at
1.82~N$\cdot$m (scaled linearly to the 16.2~N$\cdot$m human maximum) and the stress under the 3,427~N spine load. A T6
membrane model of the ring band gives its bearing and back capacities. A Tier-1 score of at least \gGateTwo{} promotes
the design to Tier~2.
\item \textbf{Tier~2, mesh and solid point.} A watertight marching-tetrahedra mesh of the voxel field is censused: no
boundary edges and no non-manifold edges. A solid quadratic-tetrahedron (TET10) model of the last 30~mm of the blade
is then solved under a 1.82~N$\cdot$m pry, on a planform mesh of at most 0.15~mm$^2$ triangles
(\code{evaluate_tier2_elites}).
\end{enumerate}
Two analytic quantities are carried unchanged into every tier's record, and Table~\ref{tab:best} labels both. The
buckling factor is an analytic estimate at every tier. The Gate-S edge geometry is measured on the exact section law
rather than on voxels. Everything else reported from the search is a Tier-1 or Tier-2 measurement.

\subsection{Islands and logging}
The islands are independent; each is seeded from its host and island numbers (\src{crates/g3/src/bin/g3_evolve.rs}{\code{g3-evolve}}).
Every 50 evaluations (\code{--log-every 50}) an island appends a \code{G3HistoryPoint} to \code{history.jsonl}. Each
point records the evaluation count, the filled niches, the Tier-2 count, the QD score, and the score, mass and tier of
the best elite (ranked by tier first, then score). At power-of-two evaluation counts and at the end, the point also
records that elite's full genome row, which Section~\ref{sec:results} renders. Logging draws no random numbers, so a
logged run produces the same archive as an unlogged one; a test checks this.
