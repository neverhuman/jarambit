\begin{abstract}
We present G4 (v\GFourVersion), a clean-slate architectural upgrade of the Jarambit evolutionary knife-design engine that resolves
the representation, physics, and optimization bottlenecks identified in the G3 v3.0.0 retrospective audit. Whereas G3 ranked
designs by an unconstrained weighted sum over 179 genes (31 of them inactive) and produced a 67.97~g finished (71.09~g as-printed)
part with two structural safety-factor shortfalls (solid tip $\mathrm{SF}=0.431$ under a global-chord 1.82~N$\cdot$m pry and
ring-clamped cantilever grip spine $\mathrm{SF}=0.515$), G4 introduces a declarative 130-gene schema (\code{genome/G4.0.toml}, zero
dead genes) coupled to a true-3D curvilinear implicit kernel, a compliant four-finger Winkler hand-contact equilibrium model
(\code{HM-1}), and a lexicographic feasibility-first quality-diversity search (\code{W\_ERGOLIGHT\_v2}) using a Feasible/Infeasible
Two-Population (\code{FI-2Pop}) Lloyd-relaxed CVT archive with a four-emitter bandit (\code{Sep-CMA-MAE}, Lamarckian
constraint-repair, feasible mass-descent, and module-aware \code{Iso+LineDD}). Every certification gate is fail-closed via
\code{G4ReleaseAdmissionContract}: three-level Roache grid-convergence index ($\mathrm{GCI}_{21} = \GFourPlateGciPct\,\% \le 5\,\%$),
per-load-case failure sequence ledger ($\min \mathrm{seq} = \GFourSfSeqMin \ge 1.10$ across lateral, torsion, and spine baton),
frame-invariant \code{TET10} solid-tip FE with a local tip-bisector diamond crown ($\mathrm{SF}_{\mathrm{LCB}} = \GFourSfTipTorsion \ge 1.25$),
2D quadratic \code{T6} serration tooth checks across all teeth including end teeth ($\GFourTeethIn/\GFourTeethIn$ inner and
$\GFourTeethSp/\GFourTeethSp$ spine teeth, $\mathrm{SF}_{\mathrm{LCB}} = \GFourSfTooth \ge 1.25$), corrected Goodman fatigue
($\mathrm{SF}_{\mathrm{LCB}} = \GFourSfFatigue \ge 1.10$), and resolution-invariant morphological erosion powder drainability. Across a
multi-seed cohort and five-arm ablation study (\code{E0}--\code{E5}), full G4 (\code{E3}) achieves a median score of
$\GFourEThreeMedScore$ (IQR $\GFourEThreeIqrScore$) and certifies winner \GFourWinnerId{} at \textbf{\GFourFinishedMassG~g}
finished mass (\GFourPrintedMassG~g as printed with bevel grind allowance; \GFourHollownessPct\,\% hollowness vs.\ its
\GFourEnvelopeMassG~g solid envelope), scoring \textbf{\GFourScoreVTwo} under \code{W\_ERGOLIGHT\_v2} (\GFourScoreVOne{} on legacy
\code{W\_ERGOLIGHT\_v1}) with zero constraint violations ($V(x)=0$) across all 29 gates.

\section{Introduction and Retrospective Audit of G3}\label{sec:intro}
Designing a one-piece, hollow Ti-6Al-4V karambit for laser powder-bed fusion (L-PBF) requires simultaneous satisfaction of
coupled geometric, ergonomic, structural, and additive-manufacturing constraints. In our preceding G3 v3.0.0 release, an
eight-island MAP-Elites search evolved a double-edged, double-serrated claw blade with an internal triply periodic minimal
surface (TPMS) lattice. A post-release red-team audit of the G3 codebase, physics pipeline, and release receipt exposed six
fundamental engineering lessons that motivate G4:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Soft scalarization permits structural shortfalls.} In \code{W\_ERGOLIGHT\_v1}, all 21 objective terms were combined
linearly through a soft knee function \code{sfk} without a feasibility barrier. Because mass carried weight $w = 0.370$ while
individual structural terms carried $0.0174$--$0.0700$, the optimizer rationally sacrificed safety factors on the solid tip
($\mathrm{SF} = 0.431$) and spine-loaded grip ($\mathrm{SF} = 0.515$) whenever thinning the geometry gained more mass points
than it lost in structural points.
\item \textbf{Boundary-condition mismatch on the grip and tip.} G3 evaluated the 3,427~N spine baton impulse on a hollow grip
cantilevered from the pommel ring ($x \approx 9.9$~mm), producing an unphysical $\sim$55~mm moment arm that does not exist when
the knife is held in a closed fist supported by the palm and four fingers~\cite{pheasant2006,garrett1971}. Similarly, G3 applied
thefull 1.82~N$\cdot$m body torque to the distal 3~mm of the point about the global origin-to-tip chord rather than the local
tip bisector.
\item \textbf{Coupled point planform and spine thickness.} In G3, the distal width profile $w(s)$ blended into the body width via
a polynomial ramp over 4~mm, coupling the 3~mm planform point angle ($\approx 92^\circ$ on the v3 winner) to the spine thickness
knots and preventing an acute $\le 55^\circ$ point from coexisting with a torsion-resistant spine.
\item \textbf{Surrogate fidelity gaps.} In G3's search loop, the plate mesh \code{mesh\_domain(\&b\_outer, \&[], 0.9)} ignored
through-blade fuller windows, the three-level Roache grid-convergence index (GCI)~\cite{roache1998} was held at a constant
placeholder $0.50$, the Goodman fatigue formula divided by capacity instead of multiplying by unit stress, the serration check
evaluated only $N-1$ interior teeth between $N$ scallops, and voxel flood-fill drainability at $h = 0.40$~mm passed single-voxel
diagonal necks that close at finer print resolutions.
\item \textbf{Genome bloat and representation ceiling.} Of the 179 genes in G3.0, 26 genes (including legacy CPPN weights and
fixed parameters) were never read by the decoder, 5 self-adaptive $\sigma$ genes were hashed into \code{genome\_sha256}, and the
blade mid-surface was strictly planar ($z \equiv 0$).
\item \textbf{Uniform-skin mass floor.} Using a uniform $\sim$1.0~mm grip skin, an unperforated longitudinal web, and a solid
pommel ring rim imposed a $\sim$60--68~g mass floor.
\end{enumerate}

G4 (\src{crates/g4/src/lib.rs}{\code{crates/g4}}) is engineered from the ground up to resolve every one of these findings while
preserving the historical v1/v2 and v3.0.0 artifacts byte-for-byte intact.

\subsection{Decoupled Point Wedge and Tip-Bisector Diamond Crown}
In G4, the planform half-width $w(a)/2$ over the distal tip zone $a \in [0, L_{\mathrm{tip,plan}}]$ follows an exact linear wedge
$w_{\mathrm{wedge}}(a) = 2 r_{\mathrm{tip}} + 2 a \tan(\theta_{\mathrm{tip}}/2)$ before transitioning via a $C^2$ quintic
smoothstep ($\tau^3(10 - 15\tau + 6\tau^2)$ over $a \ge 3.2$~mm) into the body width law. Consequently, the planform point angle
at both $a = 1.0$~mm and $a = 3.0$~mm equals $\theta_{\mathrm{tip}}$ ($\GFourPointThreeMmDeg^\circ \le 55^\circ$ on the winner)
independently of spine thickness. Meanwhile, the spine thickness over $a \in [1.5, L_{\mathrm{crown}}]$ receives a local
diamond-ridge crown $\Delta t_{\mathrm{crown}}(a) \propto \sqrt{\sin(\pi u_c)}\,t_{\mathrm{tip,crown}}$, raising the polar moment
of inertia $J \propto w\,t_{\max}^3$ by $>2.4\times$ at the cost of only $+0.19$~g of distal titanium (\code{evidence/g4/studies/e06\_tip\_crown.json}).

\section{Requirements, Hand Contact Model, and Constrained Objective}\label{sec:objective}
\subsection{Machine-Readable Specification (\code{RS-karambit-v4.toml})}
All 29 hard gates, material constants, and ergonomic target bands are loaded from \src{requirements/RS-karambit-v4.toml}{\code{RS-karambit-v4.toml}}
with \texttt{\#[serde(deny\_unknown\_fields)]} and traceable provenance in \src{requirements/PROVENANCE.md}{\code{PROVENANCE.md}}.
For each structural gate $j \in \{1,\ldots,17\}$, G4 evaluates the uncertainty-bounded lower confidence bound (LCB) safety factor:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{SF}_{j,\mathrm{LCB}}(x) = \frac{\mathrm{SF}_j(x)}{1 + k_{\mathrm{LCB}}\sqrt{\mathrm{GCI}_j^2 + \delta_{\mathrm{mat}}^2}},
\qquad k_{\mathrm{LCB}} = 1.645,
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{GCI}_j$ is the measured three-level Roache grid-convergence index and $\delta_{\mathrm{mat}} = 0.022$ is the
HIP L-PBF Ti-6Al-4V ELI yield scatter~\cite{astmf3001}.

\subsection{Unified Winkler Hand Contact Equilibrium (\code{HM-1})}
Rather than clamping the pommel ring in space while applying a 3,427~N baton impulse to the blade, G4 models the closed-fist
human grip as a four-station compliant Winkler foundation (\src{crates/g4/src/physics.rs}{\code{eval\_g4\_hand\_model\_hm1}}) at
the little/ring, middle, index-groove, and index-choil/palm-heel stations $x_i \in \{18, 39, 60, 81\}$~mm~\cite{pheasant2006,garrett1971}.
Solving the $2\times 2$ rigid-body Winkler equilibrium for each external wrench $(\mathbf{F}_{\mathrm{ext}}, \mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{ext}})$
satisfies force and moment balance to $< 10^{-12}$~N and distributes the load across the inter-finger saddle span ($l_{\mathrm{HM1}} \approx 14.5$~mm),
eliminating the artificial 55~mm ring-clamp cantilever lever arm and raising the spine-baton sequence ratio from $0.515$ (v3 ring clamp)
to $\ge 1.62$ (\code{evidence/g4/studies/e01\_v3\_rescore.json}).

\subsection{Lexicographic Feasibility-First Objective (\code{W\_ERGOLIGHT\_v2})}
G4 replaces unconstrained linear weighting with Deb's feasibility-first lexicographic ordering~\cite{deb2000}:
\begin{equation}
W_{\mathrm{ERGOLIGHT\_v2}}(x) = \begin{cases}
-1000\,V(x) + 50\,(1 - \tilde{V}(x)), & V(x) > 0, \\[4pt]
50 + 50\,\bigl(0.62\,S_{\mathrm{mass}}(x) + 0.23\,E(x) + 0.15\,S_{\mathrm{margin}}(x)\bigr), & V(x) = 0,
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
where $V(x) = \sum_{j=1}^{29} \max(0, g_j(x))^2$ is the total squared normalized constraint violation,
$S_{\mathrm{mass}}(x) = \mathrm{clip}\bigl((90 - m_{\mathrm{finished}}(x))/(90 - 28), 0, 1\bigr)$ rewards low finished mass,
$E(x) = \prod_{k=1}^{8} e_k(x)^{\beta_k}$ is the eight-factor anthropometric ergonomic geometric mean, and
$S_{\mathrm{margin}}(x)$ is a saturating reserve bonus over all 17 structural LCB margins. Any feasible design ($V(x)=0$, score
$\in [50, 100]$) strictly dominates every infeasible design ($V(x)>0$, score $< 50$).

\section{Calibrated Multi-Fidelity Physics and Verification}\label{sec:physics}
Every surrogate and certification solver in \src{crates/g4/src/physics.rs}{\code{crates/g4/src/physics.rs}} is verified by
automated tests in \src{crates/g4/tests/vv_suite.rs}{\code{vv\_suite.rs}} and R\&D study receipts in \code{evidence/g4/studies/}:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Sub-voxel smoothed-Heaviside volume integration (\code{T-MASS-1}, Study \code{E-02}):} Evaluating $H_\epsilon(\phi)$
with 8-point Gauss-Legendre sub-cell quadrature where $|\phi| \le \sqrt{3}h/2$ achieves $0.002\,\%$ volume error on a $R=10$~mm
sphere and $0.044\,\%$ on a torus at $h = 0.40$~mm ($<0.25\,\%$ drift between $h=0.40$~mm and $h=0.20$~mm).
\item \textbf{Resolution-invariant erosion drainability (\code{T-DRAIN-1}, Study \code{E-02}):} Cavity air is eroded by the physical
powder-clearance ball radius $r_{\mathrm{erode}} = 0.50$~mm ($\phi_{\mathrm{void}} \le -(r_{\mathrm{erode}} + 0.5h)$) before 6-connected
flood fill, guaranteeing identical drainability classification across $h \in \{0.40, 0.20, 0.10\}$~mm.
\item \textbf{Hole-aware plate FE and 3-level Roache GCI (\code{T-PLATE-1..2}):} Through-blade fenestrations are subtracted from the
CST+DKT sandwich plate mesh~\cite{batoz1980}, unreferenced interior hole vertices are locked, and lateral capacity is evaluated across
three refinement levels ($A_{\max} \in \{2.00, 1.00, 0.50\}\,\mathrm{mm}^2$, $r = \sqrt{2}$) to compute a real Roache
$\mathrm{GCI}_{21} = \GFourPlateGciPct\,\%$ (observed order $p = \GFourPlateOrderP$) admitted by \code{PlateGciAdmission}~\cite{roache1998}.
\item \textbf{Frame-invariant \code{TET10} solid-tip FE (\code{T-TIP-1..3}, Study \code{E-06}):} The distal 28~mm tip submodel is
transformed into the local tip-bisector frame with cell-centred thickness sampling and evaluated under \code{LC-TIP-1} (0.80~N$\cdot$m
local tip-bisector torsion) and \code{LC-TIP-2} (60~N lateral tip pry), achieving $0.000\,\%$ rigid-rotation variance.
\item \textbf{All-tooth \code{T6} serration evaluation and corrected Goodman fatigue (\code{T-SERR-4}):} Every serration tooth
$i \in \{0,\ldots,N-1\}$ on both edges---including both end teeth---is evaluated under combined 180~N snag and 60~N lateral pry
with Peterson notch sensitivity $K_f$~\cite{peterson1974} and local thickness knockdown $k_t(t_{\mathrm{local}})$, while Goodman
fatigue at $R=0$ is computed from true root bending stress $\sigma_{\max} = 180\,\sigma_{1\mathrm{N}}$.
\item \textbf{Locking-free B-bar \code{hex8} voxel FE (\code{T-VFE-1..4}):} Selective-reduced / Wilson-condensed B-bar brick
elements~\cite{simo1990} pass the constant-strain patch test to $1.1\times 10^{-16}$ and reduce thin-beam bending locking error from
$85.9\,\%$ (plain \code{hex8}) to $< 10^{-12}$.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Constrained Quality-Diversity Search (\code{FI-2Pop} + Multi-Emitter Bandit)}\label{sec:search}
\src{crates/g4/src/search.rs}{\code{crates/g4/src/search.rs}} implements a deterministic, batch-synchronous constrained QD engine:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Lloyd-relaxed 5D CVT archive (\code{FI-2Pop}):} Niche centroids in the 5D phenotypic descriptor space (finished mass
$m$, blade reach $R$, claw turn angle $\Delta\theta$, grip $H/T$, and hollowness fraction $\eta_{\mathrm{void}}$) are computed via
deterministic Lloyd relaxation~\cite{vassiliades2018}. Each cell maintains both a \emph{feasible elite} ($V(x)=0$, maximizing
$W_{\mathrm{ERGOLIGHT\_v2}}$) and an \emph{infeasible stepping stone} ($V(x)>0$, minimizing $V(x)$)~\cite{kimbrough2008}, plus a
local Pareto front over $(m_{\mathrm{finished}}, -E, -\min\mathrm{margin})$.
\item \textbf{UCB1 four-emitter bandit:} Offspring batches are generated by four specialized emitters scheduled by UCB1:
(1) \code{Sep-CMA-MAE}~\cite{fontaine2023} with diagonal covariance adaptation in $[0,1]^{130}$,
(2) a \textbf{Lamarckian constraint-repair emitter} that projects violating genes onto the active structural/geometric boundary,
(3) a \textbf{feasible mass-descent emitter} that applies coordinated mass-saving steps along Levers \code{L-1}--\code{L-8} whenever
safety margins exceed $1.15$, and
(4) a module-aware \code{Iso+LineDD} crossover emitter.
\item \textbf{Periodic $h = 0.20$~mm re-certification and cross-arch determinism:} Top archive elites are periodically re-certified at
Tier~2 ($h = 0.20$~mm, 3-level plate GCI, and 3D \code{TET10} tip FE). All transcendental math uses pure-Rust \code{libm} and
parallel batches are reduced in deterministic index order, guaranteeing bit-identical results across thread counts (\code{T-DET-1}).
\end{itemize}

\section{Scope, Limitations, and Reproducibility}\label{sec:repro}
Like G3, G4 is a computational engineering release: physical L-PBF fabrication, hot isostatic pressing (HIP), CNC bevel grinding,
and destructive mechanical testing remain required before operational qualification. All code, study receipts (\code{evidence/g4/studies/}),
cohort telemetry (\code{evidence/g4/v4.0.0/}), and the six-file manufacturing upload kit (\code{cad/}) are deterministically
reproducible via \code{cargo test --manifest-path crates/g4/Cargo.toml --release} and \code{g4-studies}, \code{g4-evolve},
\code{g4-release-tex}, and \code{g4-export}.
