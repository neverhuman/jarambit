\section{Reproducibility}\label{sec:repro}
The cohort ran at freeze commit \gCommit{} with frozen decoder hash \gDecoder. The winner's \gGeneCount-gene row is committed
at \code{evidence/g5/v\gRecord/winner\_genome.csv}; every island's logged history, the baseline island histories, the Tier-2
ranking records, and the verification JSONs under \code{evidence/g5/v\gRecord/vv/} sit alongside the single release receipt
\code{evidence/g5/v\gRecord/release.json}. \src{crates/g5/src/bin/g5_release_tex.rs}{\code{g5-release-tex}} generates every
macro in \code{docs/g5/paper/generated/}, and \src{ops/ci/gen_readme.py}{\code{ops/ci/gen\_readme.py}} generates the root
\code{README.md} and \code{cad/README.md} from the same receipt. The cohort is reproduced on the platform of record by building
\code{g5-evolve} from this release and running each island $n = 1, \ldots, \gIslands$ with \code{--init \gLaunchInit{} --evals \gEvalsIslandRaw},
\code{--seed} $4000 + n$, \code{--host \gLaunchHost{} --island} $n$, \code{--commit} \gCommit, \code{--h \gH},
\code{--tier1-top \gLaunchTierOneTop}, \code{--tier2-top \gLaunchTierTwoTop}, and \code{--log-every \gLaunchLogEvery}. The in-git
binary STL meshes and release archive files generated from the winner and cohort are listed in Table~\ref{tab:assets}.
