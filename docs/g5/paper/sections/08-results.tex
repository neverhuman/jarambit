\subsection{The continuation and its fresh-start control}
Starting from the \gPoolZeroNiches{} niches of the re-evaluated v5.0.0 elites (best \gPoolZeroBest{} on the $h = \gH$~mm search
grid), \gIslands{} warm-started islands ran \gRounds{} rounds of \gEvalsIsland{} evaluations on \gHostCount{} hosts (\gHostList;
\gEvalsTotal{} evaluations in all, at most \gRoundDepth{} rounds deep on one host). The pool lineage (\gPools{} pools, Fig.~\ref{fig:evo})
raised the best search-grid score by \gLineageGain{} to \gPoolFinalBest{}, over \gPoolFinalNiches{} niches in the final pool
\gFinalPool. The \gControlIslands{} fresh-start control islands (\gControlEvals{} evaluations each, no pool) reached at best
\gControlBest, so the lineage ended \gLineageVsControl{} the best fresh start. The winner of record \gDesign{} came from
\gWinOrigin{} (\gWinnerSearchScore{} on the search grid, \gScore{} at Tier~2 on the $h = \gHRank$~mm ranking grid).
Fig.~\ref{fig:lineage} shows the best design of pools of the lineage from a fixed camera.

\subsection{Grid check and final Tier-2 ranking}
To verify grid independence, the top designs were evaluated across five voxel pitches $h \in \{0.4, 0.3, 0.25, 0.2, 0.15\}$~mm
(Table~\ref{tab:grid}). With smoothed-Heaviside partial-volume integration and width-aware drainage, the $0.20$~mm and
$0.15$~mm grids agree within \gGridAgree\,\%, and the search's $0.40$~mm mass matches the $0.20$~mm mass of record within
\gGridSpanPct\,\%. Table~\ref{tab:top} lists the top designs from the final Tier-2 ranking (shortlist \gShortlist) at
$h = \gHRank$~mm and $0.08~\mathrm{mm^2}$ solid-tip mesh resolution; the top eight rows span \gScore--\gTopLast. Re-ranking
the shortlist at Tier~2 matters, because Tier~1 checks the tip with a section model and Tier~2 with the 3D \code{TET10} solid: the
search-grid leader \gSearchLeaderId{} (\gSearchLeaderTierOne{} at Tier~1) is at \gSearchLeaderRank{} of the Tier-2 ranking. Because the plate's lateral safety factor is not monotone in the mesh (Section~\ref{sec:measure}),
the winner is chosen on its plate cases re-solved at \gPlateCheckMeshes~mm$^2$ on the platform of record (whose production-mesh
rows reproduce the ranking bit for bit): in rank order, the first design meeting every load case on every mesh. That is
\gDesign, with worst margin $\mu = \gWorstMarginRatio$ over the meshes (\gWorstMarginProdMesh{} on the production mesh).

\subsection{The best individual (\gDesign)}
The winning individual, \gDesign{} (genome hash \gGenomeSha), scores \gScore{} under \code{W\_ERGOLIGHT\_v2} with weighted base
\gBase, hand multiplier $E = (\prod_j e_j^{\beta_j})^{\gEExp} = \gE$ ($\prod_j e_j^{\beta_j} = \gEGeo$), and worst-margin multiplier
$W = \gW$ (worst safety-factor ratio
$\mu = \gWorstMarginRatio$ on \gWorstMarginCase). Its finished partial-volume mass of record at $h = \gHRank$~mm is
\gMassFinishedG{}~g (\gMassG{}~g on the watertight print mesh; solid outer envelope \gVolEnv{}~$\mathrm{mm^3}$, \gMassEnvG{}~g).
Its grip is \gLg{}~mm long with a swell perimeter of \gSwellPerim{}~mm ($84$--$100$~mm target band) and height-to-thickness ratio
\gHoT{} ($1.5$--$1.8$ target band); the guard is \gGuardH{}~mm high and the straight-line blade reach is \gReach{}~mm. Internal
sealed void volume is \gSealedVoid{}~$\mathrm{mm^3}$ (open surface crevices \gCreviceVoid{}~$\mathrm{mm^3}$), drained through
\gPortCount{} ports of diameter \gPortD{}~mm. Because the genome scopes the evolved surface texture onto the blade
(\code{scope\_blade} $= \gScopeBlade$, depth $\gTexAmp$~mm, pitch $\gTexPitch$~mm), the as-decoded renders and print meshes show
knurling across the blade flats and bevels; the analytical edge cross-sections (Fig.~\ref{fig:edges}) and edge metrics evaluate
the bevel law restored after post-build grinding ($\gReqEdgeFinishMm$~mm finishing allowance per ground face). Fig.~\ref{fig:hero}
and Fig.~\ref{fig:edges} show its geometry;
Table~\ref{tab:best} reports all 16 structural load cases and key geometric measurements against \code{RS-karambit-v5}.

\subsection{Against the v5.0.0 winner on the same platform}
Re-evaluated on the v\gRecord{} platform of record, the v5.0.0 winner \gVFiveId{} scores \gVFiveScore{} at Tier~2 (\gVFiveTierOne{}
at Tier~1 on the search grid), with worst margin \gVFiveWorst, \gVFiveMet{} of \gCases{} load cases met and \gVFiveMass~g; its

$E = \gE$ (v5.0.0: \gVFiveE), with grip length and perimeter \gLg{} and \gSwellPerim~mm (v5.0.0: \gVFiveLg{} and \gVFivePerim~mm).

\subsection{Where the score comes from}
The score is $100\,E\,W\,\mathrm{base}$, so its 100 points split into what the design earns, what the two multipliers take and the
credit its terms fall short of (Fig.~\ref{fig:budget}). \gDesign{} earns \gBudgetEarned: structural safety \gBudgetStruct{} (the
\gCases{} load-case margins), mass \gBudgetMass, cutting geometry \gBudgetCut{} (edge, point and serrations) and build and form
\gBudgetBuild. The multipliers take \gBudgetLost{} ($E = \gE$, $W = \gW$) and \gBudgetUnmet{} is unmet credit
($1 - \mathrm{base}$). Each term's points against the most it could earn are in Fig.~\ref{fig:terms}.
