\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
Rebuilding the Jarambit pipeline around the literature-sourced requirement set \code{RS-karambit-v5} and the \code{G5.0}
decoder addressed the representation, load-case, drainage and objective flaws exposed by the G3 diagnostics. The grip
perimeter, palm-swell aspect ratio, rounded-gullet serrations, sheet-TPMS wall thickness and rib-clear drainage ports are
expressed directly in requirement units, so every candidate has a full-sized handle and width-checked drainage; the point
angle is a continuous penalty rather than a construction rule (the winner measures \gPointAngleThree\textdegree{} at
3~mm against a $\gReqPtThree^\circ$ target). In \code{W\_ERGOLIGHT\_v2} the multiplicative worst-margin factor $W$ and the
hand multiplier $E$ (the \gEExp th power of the weighted product of the hand factors) make it costly, not impossible, to trade
structural adequacy or hand comfort for grams. v\gRecord{} continued the v5.0.0 cohort on one platform of record: from the
re-evaluated v5.0.0 elites, \gIslands{} warm-started islands in \gRounds{} rounds (\gEvalsTotal{} evaluations) raised the best
search-grid score from \gPoolZeroBest{} to \gPoolFinalBest, \gLineageVsControl{} the best of \gControlIslands{} fresh-start control
islands (\gControlBest). The best individual \gDesign{} scores \gScore{} at Tier~2 ($h = \gHRank$~mm), \gVsVFiveScore{} the
v5.0.0 winner on the same platform (\gVFiveScore), weighs \gMassFinishedG{}~g (finished partial volume; \gMassG{}~g print mesh)
and meets every load case, with worst margin ratio $\mu = \gWorstMarginRatio$ as the minimum over the plate meshes
\gPlateCheckMeshes~mm$^2$ and hand multiplier $E = \gE$. Of its 100 points it earns \gBudgetEarned; the multipliers take
\gBudgetLost{} and \gBudgetUnmet{} is unmet credit. It is a computational design: printing, stress relief, grinding of the bevels
and mechanical tests against the evidence gaps of Section~\ref{sec:limits} are the next steps, before any claim of fitness for use.
